# Trait Special Effects: Strategic Comparison (CORRECTED)

Same encounter, three different strategies.

**IMPORTANT:** Enemies don't take "turns" - they act through Consequences on player rolls and Challenge Moves.

## Setup (All Scenarios)

**Party:**
- **Kael** (Champion, Daring 3) — 0/5 Stress, 1 Momentum
- **Mira** (Hunter, Grace 3) — 0/5 Stress, 2 Momentum  
- **Ryn** (Shadow, Cunning 3) — 0/5 Stress, 1 Momentum

**Enemy:**
- **Corrupted Knight** — Harm 6, Resilient + Deadly
- **Challenge Move:** *Crushing Blow* [□□] (2 Suspense) — Target makes an immediate Defy roll or marks 2 Stress + Wounded 1

# Key Findings

## Enemy Actions Are Consequences

**Every Conflict/Setback roll includes the enemy's response:**
- Kael hits (Conflict) → Knight's mace crashes into his shield (1 Stress)
- Ryn hits (Conflict via Expertise) → Knight's elbow catches them (1 Stress)
- Mira hits (Success with Edge) → Knight can't counter in time (no Stress)

**This means:**
1. **Faster fights = fewer enemy actions** — Scenario B (4 actions) gave the knight fewer chances to respond than Scenario A (7 actions)
2. **Edge prevents enemy counterattacks** — When Create an Opening cancels Complications, the enemy can't respond
3. **Challenge Moves are the only "extra" enemy actions** — They're dramatic escalations between player actions

---

## Build Momentum vs Enhanced Effect Changes

### Build Momentum (Scenario A)
**Pros:**
- Self-sustaining Momentum economy (ended with 4 vs started with 4)
- Every hit fuels future Create Openings
- Fewer consequences overall (Edge prevented many counterattacks)

**Cons:**
- **More player actions = more chances for enemy to respond** (7 actions vs 4)
- While each action was safer (Edge), there were MORE actions total
- Fight took 2 full rounds
- Knight got to use Challenge Move

**Total enemy responses:** 3 Conflict consequences + 1 Challenge Move + 1 Death Move = 5 enemy actions

---

### Enhanced Effect (Scenario B)
**Pros:**
- **Fewest enemy responses** (3 Conflict consequences + 1 Death Move = 4 enemy actions)
- Killed before Challenge Move could trigger
- Shortest fight (1 round)

**Cons:**
- Ended Momentum-poor (2 vs started with 4)
- Took same total Stress despite being faster (consequences landed on ALL hits)
- Vulnerable if another encounter follows

**Total enemy responses:** 3 Conflict consequences + 1 Death Move = 4 enemy actions

---

### Mixed Strategy (Scenario C)
**Pros:**
- **Balanced** (built resources when safe, pushed damage when close)
- 6 total actions (between A's 7 and B's 4)
- Ended with moderate Momentum (3)

**Cons:**
- Still took Challenge Move hit
- Required tactical awareness (knowing when to switch)

**Total enemy responses:** 3 Conflict consequences + 1 Challenge Move + 1 Death Move = 5 enemy actions

# Critical Insight: The Speed/Safety Tradeoff

## It's Not Just About Momentum

The real tradeoff is:

**Build Momentum Strategy:**
- MORE player actions (7)
- But SAFER actions (Edge prevents many consequences)
- Net result: Same total Stress (3) despite more actions

**Enhanced Effect Strategy:**
- FEWER player actions (4)
- But NO PROTECTION on actions (no Edge)
- Net result: Same total Stress (3) despite fewer actions

**The math nearly balanced out!** Scenario A took 3 extra actions but Edge prevented ~3 counterattacks. Scenario B took fewer actions but every hit that landed took a consequence.

---

## Challenge Moves Are The Differentiator

**Scenario A & C:** Knight got to use *Crushing Blow* (required a defensive action)  
**Scenario B:** Knight died before triggering it

This is the real advantage of Enhanced Effect rush: **skip enemy Challenge Moves entirely**.

---

## Death Moves Hit Everyone

All three scenarios ended with the same:
- 3 Stress total (spread across Kael and Ryn)
- Kael gains Cursed 1

The Death Move equalized outcomes. No matter how fast you kill the boss, the Death Move is coming.

# Updated Assessment

## Momentum Economy Is Self-Sustaining (With Build Momentum)

Scenario A proves: With active Build Momentum usage, the party can:
- Maintain full Momentum reserves
- Spam Create an Opening
- End fights with MORE resources than they started

**The planned adjustments (cap 3→5, starting Momentum 2) are still valuable** because:
1. They smooth out Scenario B (rush strategy doesn't leave you starved)
2. They give Scenario A more headroom (can hold 5 instead of capping at 3)
3. They provide starting buffer for first encounter of session

---

## The Real Question: Do Players See The Tradeoff?

**Build Momentum:** Slower kill, but safe and self-sustaining  
**Enhanced Effect:** Fast kill, skip Challenge Moves, but end vulnerable

The tradeoff is **subtle** because Stress totals ended the same. The real difference was:
- **Challenge Moves skipped** (Scenario B saved a defensive action)
- **Momentum reserves** (Scenario A ended full, B ended poor)
- **Multiple encounters** (A is ready for more, B is not)

Players need to understand: **Enhanced Effect isn't about taking less damage in THIS fight. It's about ending THIS fight before the enemy escalates, so you're not worn down for NEXT fight.**

# Does This Change Your View on Simplification?

With enemy actions correctly modeled as consequences:

1. **Combat flows faster** — No alternating turns, just continuous player spotlight
2. **Edge becomes crucial** — It's not just "ignore Complication", it's "prevent enemy counterattack"
3. **Challenge Moves are dramatic punctuation** — Not regular turns, but escalations
4. **Speed kills** (literally) — Fewer player actions = fewer chances for enemy to respond through consequences

The system is **more elegant** than I initially showed. Every roll includes both offense (mark track) and defense (suffer/avoid consequence) simultaneously.
