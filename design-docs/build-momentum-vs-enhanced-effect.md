# Trait Special Effects: Strategic Comparison

Same encounter, three different strategies.

## Setup (All Scenarios)

**Party:**
- **Kael** (Champion, Daring 3) — 0/5 Stress, 1 Momentum
- **Mira** (Hunter, Grace 3) — 0/5 Stress, 2 Momentum  
- **Ryn** (Shadow, Cunning 3) — 0/5 Stress, 1 Momentum

**Enemy:**
- **Corrupted Knight** — Harm 6, Resilient + Deadly
- **Challenge Move:** *Crushing Blow* [□□] (2 Suspense) — Target marks 2 Stress + Wounded 1

**GM starts with 3 Suspense**

---

# Scenario A: Build Momentum Strategy

**Philosophy:** "Stay safe, accumulate resources, grind them down."

## Round 1

**Kael:** Clash 3d → 2, 4, 6 (Success → Conflict from Resilient) → 1 tick (1/6), 1 Stress  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Mira

**Mira:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening: +1 Boon, +1 Edge), Clash 4d → 3, 4, 5, 6 (Success, Edge cancels Resilient) → 1 tick (2/6), 0 Stress  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Ryn

**Ryn:** Clash 3d → 2, 3, 4 (Conflict → Setback from Resilient, Expertise saves → Conflict) → 1 tick (3/6), 1 Stress  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Kael

**Enemy:** GM spends 2 Suspense for *Crushing Blow* on Kael  
**Kael:** Mira spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening for Kael), Defy 4d → 1, 3, 5, 6 (Success, Edge cancels Deadly) → Dodges clean

**End Round 1:**
- Harm: 3/6
- Party Stress: Kael 1/5, Mira 0/5, Ryn 1/5
- Party Momentum: Kael 2, Mira 1, Ryn 1 (Total: 4)
- GM Suspense: 1

## Round 2

**Kael:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 2, 4, 6, 6 (Triumph, ignores Resilient) → 1 tick (4/6), +1 Momentum (Triumph)  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Mira

**Mira:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 3, 5, 6, 6 (Triumph, ignores Resilient) → 1 tick (5/6), +1 Momentum (Triumph)  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Ryn

**Ryn:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 3, 4, 5, 6 (Success, Edge cancels Resilient) → 1 tick (6/6 DEFEATED)

**Final Tally:**
- **Rounds:** 2
- **Total Stress:** 2 (Kael 1, Ryn 1)
- **Momentum at end:** 4 (Kael 2, Mira 2, Ryn 0)
- **Strategy:** Played conservatively, accumulated resources, ended with full Momentum

# Scenario B: Enhanced Effect Strategy

**Philosophy:** "Kill it fast before it can hurt us."

## Round 1

**Kael:** Clash 3d → 2, 4, 6 (Success → Conflict from Resilient) → 1 tick (1/6), 1 Stress  
*Invokes Trait:* **Enhanced Effect (Greater)** → 2 ticks total (2/6)

**Mira:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 3, 4, 5, 6 (Success, Edge cancels Resilient) → 1 tick (3/6)  
*Invokes Trait:* **Enhanced Effect (Greater)** → 2 ticks total (5/6)

**Ryn:** Clash 3d → 2, 3, 4 (Conflict → Setback from Resilient, Expertise saves → Conflict) → 1 tick (6/6 DEFEATED), 1 Stress

**Fight ends before enemy turn!**

**Final Tally:**
- **Rounds:** 1
- **Total Stress:** 2 (Kael 1, Ryn 1)
- **Momentum at end:** 2 (Kael 1, Mira 1, Ryn 1)
- **Strategy:** Burned through the enemy before they could use Crushing Blow. GM never spent Suspense.

# Scenario C: Mixed Strategy (Adaptive)

**Philosophy:** "Read the situation, adapt each turn."

## Round 1

**Kael:** Clash 3d → 2, 4, 6 (Success → Conflict from Resilient) → 1 tick (1/6), 1 Stress  
*Decision:* "This will be a long fight, let's build resources."  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Mira

**Mira:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 3, 4, 5, 6 (Success, Edge cancels Resilient) → 1 tick (2/6)  
*Decision:* "We're doing okay, keep building."  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Ryn

**Ryn:** Clash 3d → 2, 3, 4 (Conflict → Setback from Resilient, Expertise saves → Conflict) → 1 tick (3/6), 1 Stress  
*Decision:* "Halfway there, keep resources flowing."  
*Invokes Trait:* **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Kael

**Enemy:** GM spends 2 Suspense for *Crushing Blow* on Kael  
**Kael:** Mira spends 2 Momentum (Create Opening), Defy 4d → 1, 3, 5, 6 (Success, Edge cancels Deadly) → Dodges clean

**End Round 1:**
- Harm: 3/6
- Party Stress: Kael 1/5, Mira 0/5, Ryn 1/5
- Party Momentum: Kael 2, Mira 1, Ryn 1 (Total: 4)

## Round 2

**Kael:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 2, 4, 6, 6 (Triumph, ignores Resilient) → 1 tick (4/6), +1 Momentum  
*Decision:* "We're close and have resources. Let's push for the finish."  
*Invokes Trait:* **Enhanced Effect (Greater)** → 2 ticks total (5/6)

**Mira:** Spends 1 Momentum (Create Opening), Clash 4d → 3, 5, 6, 6 (Triumph, ignores Resilient) → 1 tick (6/6 DEFEATED), +1 Momentum

**Final Tally:**
- **Rounds:** 2
- **Total Stress:** 2 (Kael 1, Ryn 1)
- **Momentum at end:** 3 (Kael 2, Mira 1, Ryn 1)
- **Strategy:** Built resources Round 1, switched to damage Round 2 when victory was in sight.

# Analysis

## Build Momentum (Scenario A)
**Pros:**
- Safest route — party ends with MORE Momentum than they started (4 vs 4 starting)
- Every hit fuels future Create Openings
- Best for long fights, multiple enemies, or when you expect more encounters

**Cons:**
- Slowest kill (6 total player actions to defeat)
- Enemy gets a turn (used Crushing Blow, forced defensive response)
- Required team coordination (passing Momentum around)

## Enhanced Effect (Scenario B)
**Pros:**
- Fastest kill (3 player actions)
- Enemy never acted (no Crushing Blow)
- Saved GM Suspense (didn't force them to spend it)

**Cons:**
- Party ends Momentum-poor (2 vs 4 starting)
- If there's another encounter immediately after, party is vulnerable
- If the fight had gone to Round 2, party would have had no resources

## Mixed Strategy (Scenario C)
**Pros:**
- Balanced (resources + speed)
- Party ends neutral on Momentum (3 vs 4 starting)
- Adapted tactically (built Round 1, pushed Round 2)

**Cons:**
- Still took 2 rounds (not as fast as Enhanced Effect rush)
- Required tactical awareness (knowing when to switch)

# Key Insights

## Build Momentum Synergizes With Create an Opening
- Every Conflict/Success hit can grant 1 Momentum to an ally
- That Momentum fuels Create an Opening for +1 Boon + 1 Edge
- Which makes future hits cleaner (fewer consequences)
- Creates a **virtuous cycle**

## Enhanced Effect Races the Clock
- Ends fights fast
- Prevents enemy Challenge Moves from triggering
- Good when:
  - Enemy has devastating moves you want to skip
  - Countdown track is ticking
  - Multiple encounters in a row (save Momentum for later)

## The Decision Point
"Do we need safety or speed?"

**Choose Build Momentum when:**
- You're taking hits and need to stabilize
- Long fight expected (high Harm track)
- You want to protect a vulnerable ally
- Multiple encounters ahead (conserve for later)

**Choose Enhanced Effect when:**
- Enemy has scary moves you want to skip
- Race against time (Countdown track)
- You're already safe (high Momentum, low Stress)
- Final encounter of the session

**Choose Mixed when:**
- You're reading the fight round-by-round
- Situation changes mid-fight
- Team has different needs (one person builds, one person pushes)

# Does This Fix the Momentum Economy?

With Build Momentum actively in play:

**Scenario A shows:** Party can sustain Create an Opening spam across multiple rounds AND end with full resources. The economy is **self-sustaining** if players prioritize Build Momentum.

**Scenario B shows:** Party can ignore Build Momentum and still win, but ends resource-starved. If there's a second encounter immediately after, they're vulnerable.

**Conclusion:** The Momentum economy works IF players use Build Momentum strategically. The planned adjustments (cap 3→5, starting Momentum 2) would make Scenario B less punishing and give Scenario A more headroom.
