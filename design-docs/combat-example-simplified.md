# Combat Example: Simplified System

## Setup

**Party:**
- **Kael** (Champion, Daring 3) — 0/5 Stress, 1 Momentum
- **Mira** (Hunter, Grace 3) — 0/5 Stress, 2 Momentum  
- **Ryn** (Shadow, Cunning 3) — 0/5 Stress, 1 Momentum

**Enemy:**
- **Corrupted Knight** (Heroic Scale)
  - Harm 6 (boss)
  - Complications: Resilient (armor), Deadly (strikes hard)
  - Challenge Move: *Crushing Blow* [□□] (2 Suspense) — Target marks 2 Stress and gains Wounded 1

**GM has 3 Suspense**

---

## Round 1

### Kael's Turn
**Kael charges the knight, Clash with Daring 3.**

**GM:** "The knight's armor is Resilient — 1 Complication. You're rolling 3 dice, and if you hit Success it'll drop to Conflict. What do you do?"

**Kael:** "I'll commit and roll."

**Rolls:** 2, 4, 6 → **Success** (one 6)

**Apply Complications:** Resilient reduces Success → **Conflict**

**Result:** Mark 1 tick on Harm (1/6), mark 1 Stress (consequence). Kael's blade scrapes armor but he takes a shield bash to the ribs.

---

### Mira's Turn
**Mira aims for a weak point, Clash with Grace 3.**

**GM:** "Still Resilient."

**Mira:** "I'll spend 1 Momentum to Create an Opening — I'm aiming for the gap in the knight's pauldron."

**Effect:** Mira now rolls with 3d + 1 Boon = 4d, and has 1 Edge.

**Rolls:** 3, 4, 5, 6 → **Success** (one 6)

**Apply Complications:** Resilient would reduce Success → Conflict, but Mira's 1 Edge cancels it.

**Result:** Success holds. Mark 1 tick on Harm (2/6). Clean hit, arrow punches through the gap. No consequence.

---

### Ryn's Turn
**Ryn circles behind and strikes, Clash with Cunning 3.**

**GM:** "The knight sees you coming — you don't have positioning advantage. Still Resilient."

**Ryn:** "I'll just roll it."

**Rolls:** 2, 3, 4 → **Conflict** (highest is 4)

**Apply Complications:** Resilient reduces Conflict → **Setback**

**GM:** "You have Expertise: Light Blades — convert Setback to Conflict."

**Result:** Mark 1 tick on Harm (3/6), mark 1 Stress (consequence). Ryn lands the strike but the knight's elbow catches them in return.

---

### Enemy Turn (GM spends 2 Suspense for Crushing Blow)

**GM:** "The knight pivots and brings their mace down at Kael with terrifying force. *Crushing Blow* activates — if this lands, you mark 2 Stress and gain Wounded 1."

**Kael:** "I Defy with Daring 3."

**GM:** "The knight is Deadly — that's 1 Complication. If you get Success, it reduces to Conflict and you'll still take 1 Stress. Do you want help?"

**Mira:** "I'll spend my last Momentum to Create an Opening for Kael — I shout a warning and fire at the knight's helmet to distract them!"

**Effect:** Kael gains +1 Boon (4d total) and +1 Edge

**Rolls:** 1, 3, 5, 6 → **Success** (one 6)

**Apply Complications:** Deadly would reduce Success → Conflict, but Kael's 1 Edge cancels it.

**Result:** Success holds. Kael ducks under the blow entirely. No consequence. *Crushing Blow* is wasted.

---

## Round 2

### Kael's Turn (Has Surge Talent)

**Kael:** "I'm going to Push Myself — activating Surge. I dig deep and unleash a flurry of strikes."

**Talent:** *Surge* — When you Clash, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and mark 2 progress instead of 1.

**GM:** "You're rolling 4 dice with Resilient still active."

**Rolls:** 2, 4, 6, 6 → **Triumph** (two 6s)

**Result:** Triumph ignores all Complications! Surge grants 2 ticks instead of 1. Mark 2 ticks (5/6), gain 1 Momentum, mark 1 Stress (Push cost). Kael's blade finds gaps repeatedly, the knight staggers.

---

### Mira's Turn

**Mira:** "Finishing shot to the visor gap. Clash with Grace 3."

**GM:** "Still Resilient."

**Rolls:** 3, 5, 6 → **Success** (one 6)

**Apply Complications:** Resilient reduces Success → **Conflict**

**Result:** Mark 1 tick (6/6 — HARM FILLED), mark 1 Stress. The arrow punches through the visor and the knight collapses.

**GM:** "The knight falls, but as they do, they grab at Kael's leg. *Death Move:* Defy or gain Cursed 2."

**Kael:** "I Defy with Daring 3."

**Rolls:** 3, 4, 5 → **Conflict**

**Result:** Mark 1 Stress (3/5 total). The curse takes hold but weakly — Cursed 1.

---

## Final Tally

**Kael:** 3/5 Stress, Cursed 1, 2 Momentum  
**Mira:** 2/5 Stress, 0 Momentum  
**Ryn:** 1/5 Stress, 1 Momentum

---

## What This Shows

### Proactive Commitment
- Mira spent Momentum **before rolling** both times (Create Opening for self, then for Kael)
- Kael committed to Push Yourself (Surge) before knowing the outcome
- No reactive "oops, let me spend Momentum to undo that" moments

### Momentum as Team Resource
- Mira's Momentum helped Kael survive Crushing Blow by Creating an Opening for him
- The party had to decide: "Who needs help more?" Not "Should I save this for myself?"

### Consequences Land
- No way to Resist after rolling Conflict/Setback
- Expertise saved Ryn from complete failure (Setback → Conflict) but didn't prevent the consequence
- Even with help, Kael took 3 Stress and Cursed 1 — heroes got hurt

### Edge vs Expertise
- Edge (from Create Opening) prevented consequences by canceling Complications **proactively**
- Expertise salvaged bad rolls **after the fact** (Setback → Conflict)
- Both were valuable at different moments

### Stress Economy
- Only marked from Consequences (6 total marks across party) and Push Yourself (1 mark)
- No spending Stress to help allies
- Stress is defensive buffer, not offensive resource (except via Talents)

### Momentum Economy  
- Started with 4 Momentum total across party
- Spent 2 (both by Mira, one for self, one for ally)
- Gained 1 from Triumph
- Ended with 3 Momentum total

**Key tension:** Mira ran out of Momentum early. If Round 2 had gone badly, she couldn't have helped anymore. The party needs more Momentum sources.
