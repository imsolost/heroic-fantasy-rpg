# Complications + Edge as One Step

## Current Framing (Wrong Order)

**Step 7: Spend Edge** (cancel Complications)  
**Step 8: Apply Complications** (reduce outcome)

**Problem:** This implies you spend Edge THEN see which Complications apply. But you need to know which Complications apply BEFORE you can decide what to cancel!

---

## Correct Order

**Step 7: Apply Complications**
1. Determine which Complication tags apply to this action
2. Spend Edge to cancel some (if you have it)
3. Reduce outcome by remaining Complications (1 tier per)

**This is really ONE step:** Resolve enemy defenses.

---

## Why These Should Be One Step

**They're not independent:**
- Edge ONLY exists to cancel Complications
- Complications ONLY get canceled by Edge
- You can't meaningfully do one without considering the other

**Analogy:** It's like saying "determine if you hit" and "apply armor" are two separate steps. They're one unified "attack resolution."

**In practice at the table:**

**Current (2 steps):**
1. "Okay, which Complications apply here?" (Step 7)
2. "I have 1 Edge, I cancel Resilient" (Step 7)
3. "Now apply remaining Complications" (Step 8)
4. "Deadly applies, so Success → Conflict" (Step 8)

**Unified (1 step):**
1. "Which Complications apply? Resilient and Deadly" 
2. "I have 1 Edge, I cancel Resilient"
3. "Deadly applies, Success → Conflict"

**Same process, but recognized as one unified step.**

## The Real Core Resolution

### Truly Irreducible Steps

1. **Frame** (establish what you're doing)
2. **Determine Pool** (count dice)
3. **Apply Modifiers** (Boons/Banes)
4. **Roll** (execute)
5. **Read Result** (determine outcome)
6. **Apply Defenses** (Complications vs Edge)
7. **Resolve** (mark tracks, consequences)

**That's 7 steps.**

**Everything else is optional:**
- Push Yourself (pre-roll resource spend) - negotiable
- Expertise (post-roll safety net) - negotiable
- Assist (ally helps) - happens during Step 3 (modifiers)

## Further Simplification: Merge Boons/Complications?

**If you wanted to go FULL Grimwild:**

**Current Archon:**
- Step 3: Boons/Banes (circumstances modify pool)
- Step 7: Complications (enemy defenses reduce outcome)
- Two separate systems

**Grimwild style:**
- Step 3: Add thorns (d8s) for ALL difficulty (circumstances + enemy defenses)
- Step 6: Check if thorns cut
- One unified system

**To match Grimwild, you'd merge:**
- Boons/Banes + Complications → Thorns
- Everything that makes success harder adds thorns
- Armored enemy = +2 thorns
- Wrong tool = +1 thorn
- Outnumbered = +1 thorn

**This would reduce to 6 steps:**
1. Frame
2. Pool
3. **Add thorns (for everything: circumstances + defenses)**
4. Roll
5. Read outcome
6. **Check if thorns cut**
7. Resolve

**But this removes:**
- Complication tags (mechanical enemy differentiation)
- Edge mechanic (tactical Complication cancellation)
- Boon/Bane clarity (circumstantial vs defensive difficulty)

**You'd lose your core identity** (tactical depth via Complications + Edge).

## My Recommendation

**Don't merge Boons + Complications.**

**Just remove Push (baseline) + Expertise:**
- 9 → 7 steps
- Same step count as Grimwild

**This is the sweet spot:**
- Simple as Grimwild (7 steps)
- Keeps tactical depth (Complications + Edge)
- Distinct identity (not just "Grimwild with different fiction")

## Consolidated Rules Text

### Apply Complications (One Step)

> **Apply Complications**
> 
> After determining your raw outcome, check which enemy Complication tags apply to this action (Resilient, Deadly, etc.). Each Complication reduces your outcome by one tier (Success → Conflict → Setback → Disaster).
> 
> **Triumph ignores all Complications** (it cannot be reduced).
> 
> **If you have Edge:** Before Complications reduce your outcome, spend Edge to cancel them (1 Edge = 1 Complication ignored). Describe how you bypass their defenses.
> 
> Apply the remaining Complications to determine your final outcome.

**This is one unified step at the table.**
