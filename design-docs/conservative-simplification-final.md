# Conservative Simplification: Remove Push Yourself (Baseline Only)

## The Change

**Remove:** Push Yourself as "+1 Boon for 1 Stress" (baseline mechanic)

**Keep:**
- Push Yourself as Talent activation framework ("mark Stress to activate special effects")
- Expertise (passive Setback conversion)
- Assist (mark Stress for ally's +1 Boon)
- Create an Opening (spend Momentum for +1 Boon + 1 Edge)
- Everything else unchanged

---

## Resolution Flow: Before vs After

### Current (9 Steps)

1. Frame
2. Pool
3. Boons/Banes
4. **Push Yourself?** (mark Stress for +1 Boon)
5. Roll
6. Raw Outcome
7. Apply Complications (with Edge)
8. Expertise (convert Setback if applicable)
9. Final Outcome

---

### After Change (8 Steps)

1. Frame
2. Pool
3. Boons/Banes
4. ~~Push Yourself removed~~
5. Roll
6. Raw Outcome
7. Apply Complications (with Edge)
8. Expertise (convert Setback if applicable)
9. Final Outcome

**9 → 8 steps (11% reduction)**

---

## What This Achieves

### 1. Cleaner Resource Separation

**Momentum (Proactive/Offensive):**
- Create an Opening (+1 Boon + 1 Edge for self)
- Resist Consequence (reduce severity)
- THE resource for "I want better odds"

**Stress (Reactive/Defensive/Supportive):**
- Absorbs hits (marks from Consequences)
- Help allies (Assist)
- Activate Talents (special abilities)
- NOT spent for generic self-boost anymore

**Before:** Both Stress and Momentum could be spent to boost your own rolls  
**After:** Only Momentum boosts your own rolls, Stress is for defense/support

---

### 2. Fewer Decision Points

**Before:** Every action, ask "Should I Push Yourself? Should I Create an Opening?"

**After:** Every action, ask "Should I Create an Opening?"

**One fewer decision per action.**

---

### 3. Talents Become More Important

**Before:** Push Yourself grants baseline +1 Boon. Talents add to this.

**After:** Push Yourself only works with Talents. No baseline benefit.

**Example Talents (how they'd be written):**

**Combat Talent:**
> **Surge:** When you Clash, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and mark 2 progress instead of 1.

**Mobility Talent:**
> **Skirmisher:** When you move in combat, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and move 1 additional zone.

**Social Talent:**
> **Silver Tongue:** When you Talk, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and choose one: learn a secret, or plant a suggestion.

**Each Talent defines what "Push Yourself" does in that context.**

---

### 4. Push Yourself as Rate Limiter (Preserved)

**Rule:**
> You can only Push Yourself once per Action Roll. If you have multiple Talents that could trigger when you Push Yourself, choose one.

**This prevents:**
- Marking 3 Stress to activate 3 Talents on one roll
- Limits power spikes
- Forces meaningful choices

**Example:**
- Character has Surge (combat) and Quick Strike (initiative) Talents
- Both could apply to this attack
- Can only Push once, so choose one Talent to activate

---

## What You Keep (Unchanged)

### Expertise

**Still works as-is:**
> When your final outcome is Setback while acting in your domain of Expertise, convert it to Conflict.

**Still provides safety net for specialists.**

---

### Assist

**Still works as-is:**
> When an ally makes an Action Roll, you may mark 1 Stress to grant them +1 Boon.

**Still allows Stress-based teamwork.**

---

### Create an Opening

**Still works as-is:**
> Before you make an Action Roll, you may spend 1 Momentum to gain +1 Boon and +1 Edge.

**Still the primary way to boost your own odds.**

---

## How Players Boost Their Own Odds (After Change)

### Pre-Roll Options

1. **Circumstantial Boons** (GM grants for good position, right tools, etc.)
2. **Create an Opening** (spend 1 Momentum → +1 Boon + 1 Edge)
3. **Assist** (ally marks 1 Stress for your +1 Boon)
4. **Talents** (mark 1 Stress via Push Yourself, if Talent applies)

**Removed: Push Yourself for generic +1 Boon**

**Still have 4 sources of Boons.** Just one fewer baseline option.

---

## Impact Analysis

### Early Game (Level 1-3)

**Characters have 2-3 Talents.**

**Before:** Could always Push Yourself for +1 Boon (universal option)

**After:** Can only Push if relevant Talent applies (contextual)

**Impact:** Slightly fewer options in situations where no Talent applies, but still have Create an Opening (Momentum) and Assist (ally).

---

### Late Game (Level 5+)

**Characters have 5-7 Talents covering various situations.**

**Before:** Push Yourself for +1 Boon, plus Talents add effects

**After:** Push Yourself activates one of many Talents (each granting +1 Boon + special effect)

**Impact:** More interesting choices (which Talent to activate?) vs. generic boost.

---

### Specialists with Expertise

**Before:** 
- Push Yourself for +1 Boon (proactive)
- Expertise converts Setback (safety net)
- Both mechanisms to avoid failure

**After:**
- Talents grant situational boosts (proactive)
- Expertise still converts Setback (safety net preserved)
- Still feel reliable

**Impact:** Minimal. Specialists still have safety net via Expertise.

---

## Rules Text Changes

### Push Yourself (New Version)

> **Push Yourself**
> 
> Some Talents allow you to Push Yourself, marking 1 Stress to activate special effects. When you do, describe how you're pushing beyond your normal limits—digging deep, ignoring pain, forcing through exhaustion.
> 
> **You can only Push Yourself once per Action Roll.** If you have multiple Talents that could trigger when you Push Yourself in this situation, choose one.
> 
> Talents that use this mechanic will say "you may Push Yourself" or "when you Push Yourself" in their text.

---

### Example Talent Rewrites

**Before:**
> **Surge:** When you Push Yourself during a Clash, you also mark 2 progress instead of 1.

**After:**
> **Surge:** When you Clash, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and mark 2 progress instead of 1.

---

**Before:**
> **Iron Will:** When you Push Yourself to resist a mental effect, you also gain 1 Edge.

**After:**
> **Iron Will:** When you resist a mental effect, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and +1 Edge.

---

### Assist (Unchanged)

> When an ally makes an Action Roll, you may mark 1 Stress to grant them +1 Boon before they roll.

---

### Create an Opening (Unchanged)

> Before you make an Action Roll, you may spend 1 Momentum to gain +1 Boon and +1 Edge.

---

### Expertise (Unchanged)

> When your final outcome is Setback while acting within your domain of Expertise, convert it to Conflict.

## Momentum Economy Impact

**Before:** Players could spend Stress OR Momentum to boost odds

**After:** Players primarily spend Momentum to boost odds (Stress is for Talents)

**Do you need more Momentum sources?**

**Current sources:**
- Triumphs (2+ sixes) - ~3-9% per roll
- Build Momentum (Special Effect) - costs progress
- Optional GM grants

**This might be okay:**
- Create an Opening is more precious (use wisely)
- Stress-based Talents provide alternative boosts (in specific contexts)
- Assist still available (teamwork)

**Monitor in playtesting.** If Momentum feels too scarce, consider:
- Increase max (3 → 4)
- More Talent sources
- Start sessions with 1 Momentum

---

## Playtesting Questions

### 1. Does Momentum Feel Scarce?

**If yes:** Increase capacity or add sources

**If no:** Current economy works

---

### 2. Do Characters Without Relevant Talents Feel Weak?

**If yes:** Ensure all starting character have 2-3 Talents covering different situations (combat, social, exploration)

**If no:** Talent variety is good

---

### 3. Is Push Yourself (Talent-Only) Clear?

**If players are confused:** Add more examples, clarify rules text

**If clear:** Good to go

---

### 4. Is Assist + Create an Opening Redundant?

**If yes:** Consider merging them later (Radical path)

**If no:** Keep separate (Stress for allies, Momentum for self)

---

## Rollback Plan

**If this change doesn't work:**

**Add back:** Push Yourself (baseline) grants +1 Boon

**Keep:** Rate limiter (only one Push per action)

**Result:** Return to current system

**Low risk because it's easy to revert.**

---

## Next Steps After Playtesting

**If this works well:**

**Consider:** Removing Expertise (next simplification step)
- Would reduce to 7 steps total
- Specialists still differentiated by higher Approach, Traits, Talents
- Further simplification without major risk

**Or consider:** Merging Assist into Create an Opening
- Unified Momentum spend (self or ally)
- Would also reduce steps
- Cleaner resource model

**But test THIS change first.**

## Summary

**Change:** Remove Push Yourself (baseline "+1 Boon for Stress")

**Keep:** Everything else (including Expertise, Assist, Create an Opening)

**Result:**
- 9 → 8 steps
- Cleaner resources (Momentum = proactive, Stress = reactive/support)
- Fewer decision points
- Talents more important
- Low risk, easy to revert

**This is the safest first step toward simplification.**

## Final Word

**This is conservative, low-risk, and achieves meaningful simplification (8 steps instead of 9).**

**It doesn't touch Expertise (safety net preserved), doesn't touch Assist (teamwork preserved), doesn't touch Complications/Edge (tactical depth preserved).**

**It just removes one overlapping resource spend (Stress for generic boost) and makes Talents the primary way to spend Stress proactively.**

**Good starting point for simplification without risking the game's identity.**
