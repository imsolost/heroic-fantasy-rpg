# Expertise = Edge: Interaction with Create an Opening

## The Change

**Current Expertise:** Converts Setback → Conflict after all other resolution  
**New Expertise:** Grants +1 Edge in your domain (cancels 1 Complication)

**Create an Opening:** Spend 1 Momentum → +1 Boon + 1 Edge

---

## Scenario 1: Specialist vs. 1 Complication Enemy

### Without Create an Opening

**Current System (2d6 + Expertise converts Setback):**
- Triumph (3%) → Triumph (ignores Complication)
- Success (31%) → Conflict (reduced by Complication)
- Conflict (25%) → Setback → **Conflict** (Expertise saves)
- Setback (44%) → Disaster → **Disaster** (Expertise can't help)

**Make progress: 59%** (3% + 31% + 25%)

---

**New System (2d6 + Expertise = +1 Edge):**
- Expertise Edge cancels the Complication → raw roll = final outcome
- Triumph (3%)
- Success (31%)
- Conflict (25%)
- Setback (44%)

**Make progress: 59%** (3% + 31% + 25%)

**Result: IDENTICAL**

---

### With Create an Opening

**Current System (2d6 → 3d6, Expertise converts Setback):**
- Triumph (9%) → Triumph (ignores Complication)
- Success (42%) → Conflict (reduced by Complication)
- Conflict (26%) → Setback → **Conflict** (Expertise saves)
- Setback (23%) → Disaster → **Disaster** (Expertise can't help)

**Make progress: 77%** (9% + 42% + 26%)

---

**New System (2d6 → 3d6, Expertise = +1 Edge, Create an Opening = +1 Edge):**
- Total: 2 Edge → cancels 1 Complication, 1 Edge left over (wasted, no second Complication)
- Raw roll at 3d6 = final outcome
- Triumph (9%)
- Success (42%)
- Conflict (26%)
- Setback (23%)

**Make progress: 77%** (9% + 42% + 26%)

**Result: IDENTICAL**

**Analysis:** Against 1 Complication, the second Edge is wasted. But the +1 Boon from Create an Opening still provides the benefit.

- Triumph (3%) → Triumph
- Success (31%) → Conflict (reduced by remaining Complication)
- Conflict (25%) → Setback
- Setback (44%) → Disaster

**Make progress: 34%** (3% + 31%)

---

- Success (42%) → Conflict → Setback → **Conflict** (Expertise saves)
- Conflict (26%) → Setback → Disaster → **Disaster**
- Setback (23%) → Disaster → **Disaster**

**Make progress: 51%** (9% + 42%)

The new system stacks Edge additively, which is much more powerful against multiple Complications.

## The Problem

**Expertise-as-Edge is worse against undefended enemies** because Edge has nothing to cancel. A master swordsman fighting an unarmored peasant would:

- Current system: Never fails (100% success, Setback → Conflict)
- New system: Fails 44% of the time (Setback stays Setback)

That feels wrong fictionally. A master shouldn't struggle against trivial opposition.

---

## Possible Solutions

### Solution 1: Expertise Grants Edge OR Converts Setback

**New Rule:**
> When you act in your domain of Expertise, you gain **+1 Edge**. If there are no Complications to cancel, you may instead **convert Setback → Conflict**.

**Pros:**
- ✅ Best of both worlds
- ✅ Effective against defended AND undefended enemies
- ✅ Maintains "unstoppable expert" fantasy

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Two different effects (more complex)
- ⚠️ "If no Complications" requires checking

**Math:**
- vs. 0 Complications: 100% success (converts Setback)
- vs. 1 Complication: 59% success (cancels via Edge)
- vs. 2 Complications: 34% success (cancels 1 via Edge)

---

### Solution 2: Expertise Grants +1 Boon Instead

**New Rule:**
> When you act in your domain of Expertise, you gain **+1 Boon**.

**Pros:**
- ✅ Simple
- ✅ Always useful (works against any number of Complications)
- ✅ Moves benefit to dice pool (cleaner resolution)

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Math changes (see earlier analysis)
- ⚠️ Less effective vs. 1 Complication (51% vs 59%)

**Math:**
- vs. 0 Complications: 77% success
- vs. 1 Complication: 51% success
- vs. 2 Complications: 51% success

---

### Solution 3: Keep Current Expertise

Maybe Expertise as "convert Setback → Conflict" isn't broken. It works against all enemy types (defended or not).

The only issue is it happens late in resolution (step 9). But is that really a problem?

---

## Create an Opening Changes

**Key finding:** With Expertise-as-Edge, **Create an Opening becomes MORE valuable against 2+ Complications**.

| Situation | Current (Expertise converts) | New (Expertise = Edge) |
|-----------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| Specialist vs 2 Complications + Create an Opening | 51% | **77%** (+26%) |

This is because Edge stacks: 1 Edge from Expertise + 1 Edge from Create an Opening = cancel 2 Complications.

**Is this good or bad?**

**Good:**
- Creates synergy between Expertise and Momentum spending
- Rewards specialists who set up their attacks
- Makes heavily-defended enemies feel conquerable

**Bad:**
- Maybe too powerful? 77% success vs. 2 Complications is quite high
- Non-specialists would be 51% in the same scenario (big gap)

---

## My Recommendation

**Go with Solution 1: Expertise grants Edge OR converts Setback (whichever applies).**

```
Expertise

When you act within your domain of Expertise:
- If facing Complications: Gain +1 Edge (cancel 1 Complication)
- If facing no Complications: Convert Setback → Conflict
```

**Why:**
- ✅ Maintains reliability against undefended enemies (100% success)
- ✅ Effective against defended enemies (cancel 1 Complication)
- ✅ Creates synergy with Create an Opening (+26% vs 2 Complications)
- ⚠️ Slightly more complex (conditional), but the condition is obvious

**Alternative simpler option:** Just keep current Expertise. It's not broken, just happens at step 9 instead of step 7. That's a minor issue.
