## Updated Core Rules

### Create an Opening (Unified Version)

> **Create an Opening**
> 
> Before any Action Roll, you may spend 1 Momentum to Create an Opening. The character making the roll gains **+1 Boon and +1 Edge**.
> 
> Describe how you're creating the advantage—tactical positioning, shouted advice, a well-timed distraction, revealing a weakness, or setting up the perfect moment.
> 
> **Only one character can Create an Opening for a given roll.** Multiple people cannot stack this benefit (it would exceed the +2 Boon cap anyway).
> 
> **Examples:**
> - For yourself: "I wait for the knight to overextend, then strike at the gap in his armor"
> - For an ally: "I shout 'Now!' and create a distraction, giving you the opening you need"

---

### Push Yourself (Talent Framework - Unchanged)

> **Push Yourself**
> 
> Some Talents require you to Push Yourself, marking 1 Stress to activate powerful effects. When you do, describe how you're pushing beyond your normal limits—digging deep through pain, forcing past exhaustion, or tapping into reserves you didn't know you had.
> 
> **You can only Push Yourself once per Action Roll.** If you have multiple Talents that could trigger when you Push Yourself in the current situation, choose one to activate.
> 
> Talents that use this mechanic will say "you may Push Yourself" or "when you Push Yourself" in their text.

---

### Assist (Removed)

~~When an ally makes an Action Roll, you may mark 1 Stress to grant them +1 Boon.~~

**This mechanic is replaced by Create an Opening (which now works for allies).**

---

### Expertise (Unchanged)

> **Expertise**
> 
> When your final outcome is Setback while acting within your domain of Expertise, convert it to Conflict. You succeed at your action, but face a Minor Consequence.
> 
> Your domains of Expertise are listed on your character sheet (Heavy Blades, Stealth, Intimidation, etc.).

## What This Achieves

### 1. Perfect Resource Separation

**Momentum (Proactive/Tactical):**
- Create an Opening (+1 Boon + 1 Edge for self OR ally)
- Resist Consequence (reduce severity)
- THE resource for "I want better odds" (for anyone)

**Stress (Reactive/Defensive):**
- Absorbs hits (marks from Consequences)
- Activate Talents (special abilities cost)
- NEVER spent for general benefits

**Clean separation:** Momentum = offense. Stress = defense.

---

### 2. Teamwork Becomes More Strategic

**Current Assist:** Multiple allies can spam Assist (each marks Stress)

**New Create an Opening:** Only one person can Create an Opening per roll (costs precious Momentum)

**Impact:**
- Teamwork is more strategic ("I'll use my Momentum to set you up")
- Less spammable (Momentum is limited)
- More impactful (+1 Edge in addition to +1 Boon)

**Example:**
> **Fighter to Rogue:** "I'll feint high to draw his attention. Use my Momentum—you get +1 Boon and +1 Edge on your backstab."

---

### 3. Simplifies Support Role

**Before:** Support characters marked Stress to Assist (separate mechanic)

**After:** Support characters spend Momentum to Create an Opening (same as everyone else)

**No special teamwork mechanic needed.** Create an Opening just works for allies naturally.

## Implications & Adjustments Needed

### Momentum Economy Must Support This

**Create an Opening now competes for Momentum:**
1. Create an Opening (self) - boost your own roll
2. Create an Opening (ally) - boost ally's roll
3. Resist Consequence - reduce harm

**Current Momentum sources:**
- Triumphs (2+ sixes) - rare
- Build Momentum (Special Effect) - costs progress
- Optional GM grants

**You MUST increase Momentum availability:**

#### Recommended Changes:

1. **Increase Capacity**
   - Current: Max 3 Momentum
   - New: Max 5 Momentum
   - Gives more room to support allies without feeling starved

2. **Add Starting Momentum**
   - Start each session with 2 Momentum
   - Ensures players always have tactical options

3. **More Talent Sources**
   - Add Talents that grant Momentum (on certain triggers)
   - Example: "When you defeat a significant enemy, gain 1 Momentum"
   - Example: "When you fill a Will track, gain 1 Momentum"

4. **Encourage GM Grants**
   - Clever plans → grant 1 Momentum
   - Excellent roleplay → grant 1 Momentum
   - Make GM grants more codified (not just "optional")

## Changes to Teamwork

### Current Teamwork Pattern

**Fighter:** "I'm attacking the knight"  
**Cleric:** "I'll Assist you" (marks 1 Stress, Fighter gets +1 Boon)  
**Rogue:** "I'll Assist too" (marks 1 Stress, Fighter gets another +1 Boon)  
**Fighter:** Rolls with +2 Boons (capped)

**Cost:** 2 Stress total (1 from each ally)  
**Benefit:** +2 Boons for Fighter

---

### New Teamwork Pattern

**Fighter:** "I'm attacking the knight"  
**Cleric:** "I'll Create an Opening for you" (spends 1 Momentum, Fighter gets +1 Boon + 1 Edge)  
**Rogue:** "I can't—only one Opening per roll"

**Cost:** 1 Momentum total  
**Benefit:** +1 Boon + 1 Edge for Fighter (arguably better than +2 Boons)

---

### Analysis

**Old way:** Cheaper (Stress regenerates), spammable (multiple allies), weaker per helper (+1 Boon each)

**New way:** More expensive (Momentum is scarce), limited (one person only), stronger (+1 Boon + 1 Edge)

**Trade-off:** Less teamwork spam, more strategic teamwork.

**This is GOOD.** Makes "who spends Momentum to help?" a tactical decision rather than "everyone Assists if they have Stress."

## Updated FAQ

### Q: Can multiple people Create an Opening for the same roll?

**A: No.** Only one Create an Opening can apply to each Action Roll (the first person to declare it).

**Why:** It's already powerful (+1 Boon + 1 Edge), the Boon cap is ±2 anyway, and we want one strategic choice per roll rather than everyone piling on.

---

### Q: Can I Create an Opening for myself and have an ally Create an Opening for me too?

**A: No.** Only one Create an Opening per roll, period. Whether it's you spending your own Momentum or an ally spending theirs, only one can apply.

---

### Q: What if I want to help an ally but don't have Momentum?

**A: You have other options:**
- Position yourself to create circumstantial Boons (GM grants)
- Use a Talent if you have one (Push Yourself)
- Take an action that makes their next action easier (setup)

**Or:** Generate Momentum first (roll a Triumph, use Build Momentum, etc.) then help on their next roll.

---

### Q: Can I Create an Opening for an ally who's in a different zone/area?

**A: Yes, if it makes fictional sense.** 

- Shouting tactical advice works from a distance
- Revealing information works remotely
- Creating a distraction can work across zones

**But:** Some Creating an Opening setups require proximity (physical positioning, handing them tools, etc.). GM adjudicates based on fiction.

---

### Q: How does this interact with Teamwork (3+ people on one task)?

**From Chapter 4:**
> When 3 or more PCs work together on a single task, one person makes the primary roll. Others may Assist (mark 1 Stress each), subject to the +2 Boon cap.

**Update this to:**
> When 3 or more PCs work together on a single task, one person makes the primary roll. One other person may Create an Opening for them (spend 1 Momentum for +1 Boon + 1 Edge).

**Teamwork is now:**
- One person rolls
- One person can Create an Opening
- Others can use Talents (Push Yourself) if they have relevant ones
- Or create circumstantial Boons through positioning

## Momentum Economy Guidelines

### How Much Momentum is Enough?

**Players should have enough Momentum to:**
- Create an Opening 1-2 times per session (for themselves)
- Create an Opening for an ally once per session
- Resist Consequence 1-2 times per session

**That's 3-5 Momentum spends per session per player.**

**At max 5 Momentum capacity:**
- Start with 2
- Gain 2-3 during session (Triumphs, Talents, GM grants)
- Spend 3-5 across the session
- End around 0-2

**This feels about right.** Momentum is precious but available.

---

### Recommended Momentum Sources (Beyond Triumphs)

#### 1. Session Start
> Each player starts the session with 2 Momentum.

#### 2. Talent Sources (Examples)

> **Momentum on Victory:** When you reduce an enemy's track to 0, gain 1 Momentum.

> **Momentum on Breakthrough:** When you fill an Obstacle track, gain 1 Momentum.

> **Momentum on Conviction:** When you fill a Will track through Talk (not Clash), gain 1 Momentum.

> **Momentum on Discovery:** When you learn a significant secret or solve a mystery, gain 1 Momentum.

#### 3. GM Grants (Codified)

**Grant 1 Momentum when:**
- Player executes a clever plan that wasn't on anyone's radar
- Player creates a dramatic moment that energizes the table
- Player's roleplay reveals character depth in a meaningful way
- The group overcomes a major challenge or completes a story arc

**Don't grant Momentum for:**
- Just doing what the rules say
- Generic "good idea" stuff
- Constantly (devalues it)

**Aim for 1-2 GM grants per session, distributed across the group.**

**This requires Momentum economy tuning:**
- Must increase capacity (5)

- Cleaner design
