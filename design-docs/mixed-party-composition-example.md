# Mixed Party Composition Example

**Party with One Fighter:**

## Setup

**Party:**
- **Kael** (Champion, Daring 3) — Combat specialist, 0/5 Stress, 2 Momentum
- **Lyra** (Shadow, Cunning 2, Clash 1d, Sense 3d) — Scout, 0/5 Stress, 2 Momentum  
- **Finn** (Sage, Spirit 2, Clash 1d, Know 3d, Defy 2d) — Scholar, 0/5 Stress, 2 Momentum

**Enemy:**
- **Corrupted Knight** — Harm 6, Resilient + Deadly
- **Challenge Move:** *Crushing Blow* [□□] (2 Suspense)

**GM starts with 3 Suspense**

---

## What Changes With This Party?

**Only Kael can reliably fight:**
- Kael (3d Clash) vs Resilient: ~35% Success before Complications
- Lyra (1d Clash) vs Resilient: 17% Success → reduced to Conflict = bad odds
- Finn (1d Clash) vs Resilient: Same as Lyra

**If Lyra or Finn Clash, they'll likely:**
- Roll Setback (50% chance) → fail completely
- Roll Conflict (33% chance) → Resilient reduces to Setback → fail
- Even Success (17% chance) → Resilient reduces to Conflict → mark 1 tick but take 1 Stress

**Better strategy: Use their strengths**

---

## Round 1: Tactical Approach

### Lyra (Shadow with Sense 3d)
**Action:** "I'm circling the knight, looking for weaknesses in their armor."  
**Sense with Cunning 2 + Trait ("Shadow of the Alleys") = 3d**  
**Roll:** 3d → 3, 5, 6 (Success)  
**Outcome:** Learn a critical detail: "The knight's left pauldron has a gap where the leather straps are frayed."  
**GM:** "That weakness grants anyone targeting it **1 Edge** on their next Clash."  
**Invokes Trait:** **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Kael

**Party Status:** Kael 3 Momentum | Lyra 2 Momentum | Finn 2 Momentum

---

### Kael (Champion with Clash 3d)
**Action:** "I exploit the gap Lyra found!"  
**Spends:** 1 Momentum to Create an Opening  
**Benefit:** +1 Boon (4d total) + 1 Edge (from Create Opening) + 1 Edge (from Lyra's discovery) = **2 Edge total**  
**Roll:** 4d → 2, 4, 5, 6 (Success)  
**Apply Complications:** Resilient (1) + Deadly (1) = 2 Complications total, but 2 Edge cancels both  
**Outcome:** Success stays Success. Blade punches through the gap (1 tick, now 1/6). Knight can't counter through all that Edge.  
**Invokes Trait:** **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Finn

**Party Status:** Kael 2 Momentum | Lyra 2 Momentum | Finn 3 Momentum

---

### Finn (Sage with Know 3d)
**Action:** "I'm recalling what I know about corrupted knights from the Archives."  
**Know with Spirit 2 + Trait ("Scholar of the Forbidden") = 3d**  
**Roll:** 3d → 4, 5, 6 (Success)  
**GM:** "You know the corruption magic animating them is vulnerable to disruption. If someone can **Defy** to disrupt the magic when it flares, it'll remove the Deadly Complication for the rest of the fight."  
**Invokes Trait:** **Build Momentum** → Grants 1 Momentum to Lyra

**Party Status:** Kael 2 Momentum | Lyra 3 Momentum (capped at 2) | Finn 2 Momentum

---

### GM Challenge Move
**GM:** "The knight's corruption flares — dark magic crackles around its blade. *Crushing Blow* is coming at Kael!"  
**Spends:** 2 Suspense

**Finn:** "I'm disrupting the magic! Defying with Spirit!"  
**Action:** Defy with Spirit 2  
**Roll:** 2d → 4, 6 (Success)  
**Outcome:** The corruption magic sputters and fades. The knight's blade loses its deadly edge.  
**GM:** "The knight's **Deadly** Complication is removed for the rest of the fight. The *Crushing Blow* still comes, but without the magical enhancement."

**Kael:** "I'm dodging!"  
**Action:** Defy with Daring 3  
**Lyra:** "Creating an Opening for him!" (Spends 1 Momentum)  
**Roll:** 4d → 2, 3, 5, 6 (Success)  
**Apply Complications:** (Deadly is removed, no Complications apply)  
**Outcome:** Kael rolls clear easily.

**Party Status:** Kael 2 Momentum | Lyra 1 Momentum | Finn 2 Momentum  
**Enemy Status:** 1/6 Harm, **Resilient only** (Deadly removed)

---

## Round 2: Press the Advantage

### Lyra
**Action:** "I'm not fighting the knight directly — I'm creating a distraction so Kael can land the killing blow."  
**Spends:** 1 Momentum to Create an Opening for Kael  
**Narrative:** Lyra throws a dagger at the knight's visor, forcing them to flinch

**Party Status:** Kael 3 Momentum (2 from before + 1 from Lyra's Opening) | Lyra 0 Momentum | Finn 2 Momentum

---

### Kael
**Action:** Clash with Daring 3  
**Spends:** 1 Momentum to Create an Opening (own)  
**Benefit:** +1 Boon (4d) + 1 Edge (from Create Opening) + 1 Edge (from Lyra's Create Opening) = **2 Edge total**  
**Roll:** 4d → 3, 6, 6, 6 (Triumph - triple 6s!)  
**Apply Complications:** Triumph ignores all Complications anyway  
**Outcome:** Massive strike through the weakened armor (1 tick, now 2/6). Gain 1 Momentum from Triumph.  
**Invokes Trait:** **Enhanced Effect (Greater)** → 2 ticks total (now 3/6)

**Party Status:** Kael 3 Momentum | Lyra 0 Momentum | Finn 2 Momentum

---

### Finn
**Action:** "I'm staying out of melee range and preparing to shield us if needed."  
**Holds action, ready to Defy if the knight counterattacks**

---

### Kael (Spending Momentum Aggressively)
**Action:** "Finishing blow!"  
**Spends:** 1 Momentum to Create an Opening  
**Roll:** 4d → 2, 4, 5, 6 (Success)  
**Apply Complications:** Resilient would reduce to Conflict, but 1 Edge cancels it  
**Outcome:** Success holds (1 tick, now 4/6)  
**Invokes Trait:** **Enhanced Effect (Greater)** → 2 ticks total (now 6/6 - **DEFEATED**)

---

## Final Tally

- **Total Player Actions:** 6 (3 Round 1 non-combat + 1 Defy + 4 Round 2)
- **Total Stress:** 0 (nobody took damage)
- **Afflictions:** None
- **Momentum at end:** Kael 2, Lyra 0, Finn 2

---

## What This Shows

### 1. **Non-Fighters Should Avoid Direct Combat**

**Lyra and Finn never Clashed.** Instead:
- **Lyra used Sense** → Discovered weakness (granted Edge)
- **Finn used Know** → Learned how to remove Deadly
- **Finn used Defy** → Actually removed Deadly
- **Both used Create an Opening** → Boosted Kael's attacks

If Lyra had Clashed with 1d:
- 50% chance of Setback → fail, mark 2 Stress (Major Consequence)
- 33% chance of Conflict → reduced to Setback by Resilient → fail anyway
- Even on Success → reduced to Conflict → mark 1 tick but take 1 Stress

**Clashing with 1d is a trap for non-fighters.**

---

### 2. **Support Actions Stack**

Kael's big attack had:
- **4d pool** (Daring 3 + 1 Boon from Create Opening)
- **2 Edge** (1 from own Create Opening + 1 from Lyra's Create Opening)
- Against enemy with only **Resilient** (Deadly removed by Finn)

This let Kael punch through effortlessly. **Multiple characters supporting one attacker is often better than everyone attacking separately.**

---

### 3. **Sense and Know Are Combat Actions**

- **Sense:** Discovered weakness → granted Edge to allies
- **Know:** Learned how to remove Complication → enabled Finn's Defy

These aren't just exploration actions. **They're tactical combat moves that remove/bypass enemy defenses.**

---

### 4. **Role Differentiation Emerges Naturally**

**Fighter (Kael):**
- Deals damage
- Receives support from allies
- Manages Momentum to sustain offense

**Scout (Lyra):**
- Uses Sense to find weaknesses
- Creates Openings for fighter
- Stays out of direct combat

**Sage (Finn):**
- Uses Know to learn enemy mechanics
- Uses Defy to disrupt/protect
- Creates Openings for fighter

**Nobody felt useless.** Everyone contributed meaningfully without needing to Clash.

---

### 5. **Party Took Zero Stress**

By using support actions and information gathering:
- Lyra's Sense granted Edge
- Finn's Know revealed how to remove Deadly
- Finn's Defy removed Deadly
- Create Openings stacked Edge on Kael's attacks
- Kael's attacks never reduced below Success (no consequences)

**This party was MORE EFFECTIVE than the "everyone Clashes" party** despite having fewer combat-capable members.

---

## Alternative Scenario: If They All Clashed Anyway

### Round 1 (Bad Strategy)

**Lyra:** Clash 1d → 3 (Conflict) → Resilient reduces to Setback → Fail, mark 2 Stress  
**Kael:** Clash 3d → Success → Resilient reduces to Conflict → 1 tick, mark 1 Stress  
**Finn:** Clash 1d → 2 (Setback) → Fail, mark 2 Stress

**Result After Round 1:**
- Harm: 1/6 (only Kael contributed)
- Party Stress: Kael 1/5, Lyra 2/5, Finn 2/5
- Party is taking massive casualties for minimal progress

**This would take 6+ rounds and the party would be near-Taken Out by the end.**

---

## Key Takeaway

**Archon rewards using your strengths, not forcing every character into the same role.**

If your character has:
- **Low Clash:** Use Sense/Know/Defy/Talk instead
- **High Sense:** Discover weaknesses (grant Edge)
- **High Know:** Learn enemy mechanics (enable counterplay)
- **High Defy:** Protect allies, disrupt enemies
- **High Talk:** Reduce enemy Will tracks (if applicable)

**Support actions + one good attacker > multiple mediocre attackers**

The system naturally creates:
- Tanks (high Clash + high Defy)
- Scouts (high Sense + Create Openings)
- Sages (high Know + problem-solving)
- Faces (high Talk + social encounters)

Without needing class-based restrictions or "healer/DPS/tank" roles hardcoded into the mechanics.
