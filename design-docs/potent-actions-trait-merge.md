# Potent Actions & Trait Merge — September 2026

## Summary

Simplified Potent Actions by merging with Trait system. Potent Actions now grant a free Trait Effect (Enhanced/Additional/Build Momentum) instead of separate mechanics (Edge+1, Scale Enhancement, etc.). Renamed Greater/Secondary Effect → Enhanced/Additional Effect for clarity.

---

## Problem Statement

**Redundancy between Potent Actions and Traits:**
- Potent Actions had 4 separate benefits (Edge+1, Expanded Possibility, Narrative Permission, Scale Enhancement)
- Traits had 3 Special Effects (Greater/Secondary/Build Momentum)
- Both systems granted similar outcomes (bigger effects, tactical flexibility, resource generation)
- Terminology inconsistency (Greater vs. Enhanced, Secondary vs. Additional)
- Teaching burden: explain Potent Actions fully, then explain Traits fully, then explain how they stack

**Specific issues:**
1. **Edge+1 overlap:** Potent granted Edge, but so does Create an Opening (Momentum spend). Unclear when to use which.
2. **Scale Enhancement redundancy:** "Heightened Impact" and "Greater Effect" both mean "bigger result." Unnecessary duplication.
3. **Failure case:** If Potent amplified your chosen Trait Effect but no Trait applied, Potent gave minimal payoff despite Stress cost.

---

## Solution: Potent Grants Free Trait Effect

**New Potent Actions:**

1. **Expanded Possibility** — Attempt the Impossible (with Banes) OR exceed Scale ceiling
2. **Free Trait Effect (Conflict+)** — Choose one: Enhanced, Additional, or Build Momentum (no relevant Trait required)
3. **If relevant Trait also applies:** Choose a second different Trait Effect (stacking)

**Terminology cleanup:**
- Greater Effect → stays as sub-option of **Enhanced Effect** (Greater or Broad)
- Secondary Effect → renamed to **Additional Effect**
- Build Momentum → unchanged

---

## Design Rationale

### Why Merge?

**1. Eliminates redundancy:**
- Old: Potent had "Scale Enhancement" (3 dimensions: Heightened/Widespread/Extended)
- Old: Traits had "Greater Effect" (+1 tick/question/etc.)
- **New:** Both use Enhanced Effect (Greater = heightened impact, Broad = widespread)

**2. Solves failure case:**
- Old: Potent amplifies your Trait choice → if no Trait applies, minimal payoff
- **New:** Potent grants Trait Effect regardless → always pays off

**3. Creates interesting combos:**
- When both Potent and Trait apply, pick two different effects
- All combinations are valid: Greater+Additional, Broad+Build, etc.
- Players optimize builds around favorite combos

**4. Clearer resource economy:**
- **Momentum (Create an Opening):** +1 Boon +1 Edge (helps you land the action)
- **Stress (Push Yourself for Potent):** Free Trait Effect + Expanded Possibility (makes success bigger/possible)

**5. Simpler teaching:**
- "Potent lets you pick a Trait Effect without needing a relevant Trait"
- "If you do have a relevant Trait, pick two different effects"

---

### Why Drop Edge+1?

**Original Potent granted Edge+1.** In the new version, it doesn't.

**Reasons to drop:**
1. **Overlap with Create an Opening:** Momentum already grants Edge. If Potent also grants Edge, resources feel redundant.
2. **Simpler:** Potent becomes 2 benefits (Expanded + Trait Effect) instead of 3 (Edge + Expanded + Trait Effect)
3. **Clear differentiation:** Momentum = tactical advantage (Boon+Edge), Stress = heroic scale (Trait Effect + Expanded)
4. **Interesting choice:** Should I spend Momentum for Edge first, then Push Yourself? Or gamble on raw Potent?

**Tradeoff:** Against 2+ Complication enemies, Potent Actions become riskier without guaranteed Edge. **This is intentional** — forces players to think tactically (generate Edge via Momentum first, or accept risk).

---

### Why "Additional" instead of "Secondary"?

**Terminology consistency:**
- "Enhanced" and "Additional" are clearer parallel terms than "Greater" and "Secondary"
- "Secondary" implies "lesser" or "backup" — doesn't match the power level (free Defy is strong)
- "Additional" accurately describes what it is: an additional action

---

## Comparison to Grimwild

**Grimwild Potency:**
- Primarily about Vantage manipulation (move on difficulty scale: Impossible → Doubtful → Reasonable)
- Narrative, flexible: "can be more intense or broad, create dramatic opportunities, bend the rules"
- Examples show various outcomes but no rigid menu

**Our Potency:**
- Primarily about outcome enhancement (what you can choose when you succeed)
- Structured menu: pick Enhanced, Additional, or Build Momentum
- Expanded Possibility handles the "attempt the impossible" part (like Grimwild's Vantage manipulation)

**Why we're different:**
- Our system already uses structured menus (Trait Effects, Momentum spends)
- Grimwild uses more narrative negotiation throughout
- Our approach fits our design philosophy better (clear options, player agency, predictable outcomes)

## Gameplay Implications

### Before (Separate Systems)

**Potent Talent usage:**
> Champion uses "Unstoppable Force" → mark 1 Stress
> - Gain Edge+1 (ignore Complication)
> - Gain Scale Enhancement (choose Heightened Impact)
> - If Trait also applies: separately choose Greater/Secondary/Build
>
> **Result:** Two separate systems firing, unclear how they interact

### After (Unified)

**Potent Talent usage:**
> Champion uses "Unstoppable Force" → mark 1 Stress
> - Choose one Trait Effect from Potent: **Enhanced (Greater)**
> - If Trait also applies: choose different Trait Effect: **Additional Effect**
>
> **Result:** Two Trait Effects from same roll (one from Potent, one from Trait)

### Combo Builds

**Offense Specialist:**
- Optimize for Enhanced (Greater) + Additional Effect combos
- Maximize ticks marked + free tactical Defy actions
- Build around Potent Talents that activate frequently

**Support Specialist:**
- Optimize for Enhanced (Broad) + Build Momentum combos
- Affect multiple allies + generate Momentum for team
- Build around Potent Talents that help allies

**Flex Player:**
- Switch between combos based on situation
- Greater+Additional against bosses (damage + control)
- Broad+Build in group fights (spread effects + resources)

## Examples in Play

### Example 1: Offense Combo (Greater + Additional)

> **Round 1:** Champion Clashes armored knight (4 HP, Resilient + Deadly).
>
> **Setup:**
> - Pool: 3d6
> - No Edge (didn't spend Momentum)
> - "Unstoppable Force" Talent grants Potency
>
> **Resolution:**
> - Roll: Success (6)
> - Apply Complications: Success → Conflict → Setback
> - "War-Hardened" Trait applies
> - **From Potent:** Choose Enhanced (Greater) → would mark +1 tick BUT Setback = 0 ticks marked
> - Wait, we're at Setback, so no Trait Effects at all!
> - Mark 2 Stress (Major Consequence + Push Yourself = 3 Stress total, but Talent waives Push cost = 2 Stress)
>
> *This shows the risk without Edge — 2 Complications cascade to Setback despite Success roll.*
>
> **Round 2:** Champion spends 1 Momentum for Create an Opening (Edge+1), then Clashes again with Potent.
> - Roll: Success (6)
> - Apply Edge+1: ignore Resilient → Success → Conflict (Deadly still applies)
> - **From Potent:** Choose Enhanced (Greater) → mark 2 ticks (1 base + 1 Greater)
> - **From Trait:** Choose Additional Effect → free Defy to shove knight backward
> - Mark 1 Stress (Conflict consequence)
> - **Result:** 2 HP damage + tactical advantage + knight pushed back

### Example 2: Support Combo (Broad + Build)

> **Mage** casts ward on party as dragon breathes fire.
>
> **Setup:**
> - Pool: 3d6
> - "Potent Spell" Talent grants Potency
> - GM rules protecting entire party is normally Impossible (can only shield self)
>
> **Resolution:**
> - Roll: Success (6)
> - Expanded Possibility made it attemptable
> - "Arcane Prodigy" Trait applies
> - **From Potent:** Choose Enhanced (Broad) → shield covers all nearby allies
> - **From Trait:** Choose Build Momentum → grant 1 Momentum to party pool
> - Mark 1 Stress (Push Yourself)
> - **Result:** Party-wide protection + 1 Momentum for team

### Example 3: No Trait Applies (Potent Still Works)

> **Champion** breaks down magically sealed door (no relevant Trait for magic).
>
> **Setup:**
> - Pool: 3d6
> - "Unstoppable Force" Talent grants Potency
> - GM rules breaking magical seal is Impossible
>
> **Resolution:**
> - Roll: Conflict (4-5)
> - Expanded Possibility made it attemptable
> - No relevant Trait
> - **From Potent:** Choose Additional Effect → free Defy to seize control of room beyond
> - Mark 1 Stress (Conflict + Push = 2 total)
> - **Result:** Door broken + immediately dominate the space inside

## Valid Trait Effect Combinations

When both Potent and Trait apply (pick two different):

| Potent Choice | Trait Choice | Result |
|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Enhanced (Greater) | Additional Effect | +1 tick/question + free Defy action |
| Enhanced (Greater) | Build Momentum | +1 tick/question + 1 Momentum |
| Enhanced (Broad) | Additional Effect | Affect small group + free Defy action |
| Enhanced (Broad) | Build Momentum | Affect small group + 1 Momentum |
| Enhanced (Greater) | Enhanced (Broad) | +1 tick/question AND affect small group |
| Additional Effect | Build Momentum | Free Defy action + 1 Momentum |

**Invalid combinations (same effect twice):**
- Enhanced (Greater) + Enhanced (Greater)
- Additional Effect + Additional Effect
- Build Momentum + Build Momentum

## Migration Notes

### Talents That Grant Potency

**No mechanical changes needed** — Talents still grant Potency the same way (usually via Push Yourself). The only difference is what Potency does when you use it.

**Example Talent (unchanged):**
> **Unstoppable Force:** When you Clash or Defy with Daring, you may Push Yourself to gain Potency. Mark 1 Stress after resolving the roll.

**What changed:** The benefit of Potency (used to be Edge+Scale, now is Trait Effect+Expanded).

### Players Need to Learn

**Old mental model:**
- "Potent gives me Edge and Scale Enhancement"
- "Traits give me Greater/Secondary/Build"

**New mental model:**
- "Potent lets me pick a Trait Effect without needing a Trait"
- "If I have both, I get two different Trait Effects"

**Teaching script:**
> "When you Push Yourself for a Potent Action, you can pick any Trait Effect—Enhanced, Additional, or Build Momentum—even if no Trait applies. If you also have a relevant Trait, you pick a second different effect. Think of Potent as unlocking Trait-level results even when your character identity doesn't directly apply."

## Design Trade-offs

### What We Gained

✅ **Simpler system:** 2 mechanics instead of 4 (Edge+Expanded+Scale+Permission → Expanded+Trait Effect)  
✅ **No failure case:** Always get Trait Effect from Potent (no Trait required)  
✅ **Better stacking:** Two Trait Effects is clearer than "Potency amplifies your chosen effect"  
✅ **Clear resource differentiation:** Momentum=tactical, Stress=heroic scale  
✅ **Clearer terminology:** Enhanced/Additional parallel structure  
✅ **Easier teaching:** "Potent = free Trait Effect" is one sentence

### What We Lost

❌ **Edge+1:** Potent no longer helps land the action (makes 2+ Complication enemies riskier)  
❌ **Explicit Scale dimensions:** No longer teach Heightened/Widespread/Extended as separate choices (now part of Expanded Possibility description)

### What Stayed the Same

✔️ **Heroic scope:** Can still attempt impossible feats (Expanded Possibility)  
✔️ **Stress cost:** Still costs 1 Stress via Push Yourself  
✔️ **Power level:** Two Trait Effects (Potent+Trait) ≈ old system's combined benefits  
✔️ **Talent activation:** Talents still grant Potency the same way
