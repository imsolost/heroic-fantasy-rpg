# Push Yourself Redesign: Post-Roll Conversion

## The Proposal

**Instead of:** Mark 1 Stress BEFORE roll for +1 Boon (Step 4)  
**New:** Mark 1 Stress AFTER roll to convert Setback → Conflict (Step 9)

This would replace both current Step 4 (Push Yourself) and Step 9 (Expertise) with a single post-roll decision.

---

## How It Works

**Current Resolution:**
1. Frame
2. Pool
3. Boons/Banes
4. **Push Yourself? (mark Stress for +1 Boon)** ← pre-roll
5. Roll
6. Raw Outcome
7. Edge
8. Complications
9. **Expertise (passive Setback conversion)** ← post-roll
10. Final Outcome

**Proposed Resolution:**
1. Frame
2. Pool
3. Boons/Banes
4. ~~Push Yourself removed~~
5. Roll
6. Raw Outcome
7. Edge
8. Complications
9. **Push Yourself (active Setback conversion, costs 1 Stress)** ← replaces Expertise
10. Final Outcome

**Result: Effectively 9 steps** (Step 4 disappears, Step 9 changes from passive to active)

---

## Comparison to Grimwild's Push

**Grimwild Push:**
- Roll diminishing pool
- If no dice removed (failure), mark harm to force 1d removal
- Turns "no progress" into "some progress"
- Happens post-roll, costs resource

**Archon Proposed Push:**
- Roll action
- If Setback (failure), mark Stress to convert to Conflict
- Turns "failure" into "success-at-cost"
- Happens post-roll, costs resource

**These are very parallel!** Grimwild's Push is also post-roll modification.

---

## What Changes?

### Decision Timing

**Current:** "Should I spend Stress NOW to improve my odds?"  
**Proposed:** "I failed. Should I push through anyway?"

**Proposed is more dramatic:**
- Pre-roll Push feels cautious/preparatory ("I'm digging deep")
- Post-roll Push feels desperate ("I won't give up!")
- Narratively stronger moment

---

### Resource Tension

**Current:** Spend Stress to avoid failure  
**Proposed:** Spend Stress to convert failure

**Proposed creates clearer trade-off:**
- You KNOW you failed before deciding
- Not gambling Stress on better odds
- Direct exchange: 1 Stress = convert this failure

---

### Who Can Use It?

**Current System:**
- Push Yourself: Everyone (+1 Boon for 1 Stress)
- Expertise: Only specialists (passive Setback conversion)

**Proposed System:**
- Push Yourself: Everyone (active Setback conversion for 1 Stress)
- Expertise: ~~Removed~~

**Everyone gets access to the conversion**, not just specialists.

---

## The Specialist Problem

**If everyone can Push to convert Setback, what makes specialists special?**

### Option 1: Specialists Get Free Push (In Domain)

**Everyone else:** Mark 1 Stress to Push  
**Specialists:** Can Push for FREE in their domain

**Example:**
- Rogue (Expertise: Stealth) fails a stealth roll
- Converts Setback → Conflict without marking Stress
- Non-specialists must mark Stress to do the same

**Narrative:** Masters push through obstacles in their specialty naturally.

---

### Option 2: Specialists Get +1 Edge (In Domain)

**Everyone else:** Can Push to convert Setback  
**Specialists:** Get +1 Edge in domain (cancel 1 Complication at Step 7)

**Example:**
- Warrior (Expertise: Heavy Blades) attacks armored knight (Resilient)
- Auto-cancels Resilient via Edge
- Faces cleaner roll, less likely to need Push

**Narrative:** Masters bypass defenses, so they fail less often.

---

### Option 3: Specialists Get +1 Boon (In Domain)

**Everyone else:** Can Push to convert Setback  
**Specialists:** When acting in domain, automatically add +1 Boon to pool (Step 2)

**Example:**
- Scholar (Expertise: Arcana) researches ancient spell
- Rolls with +1 Boon automatically
- Higher success rate, less likely to need Push

**Narrative:** Masters are more competent, so they fail less often.

---

### Option 4: Specialists Can Upgrade Push

**Everyone else:** Push converts Setback → Conflict  
**Specialists:** In domain, Push converts Conflict → Success (or Setback → Success)

**Example:**
- Thief (Expertise: Lockpicking) gets Conflict on complex lock
- Pushes to convert Conflict → Success (clean success, no consequence)
- OR: Gets Setback, Pushes to convert Setback → Success (skips Conflict)

**Narrative:** Masters can turn near-failures into clean successes.

---

## Analysis of Options

| Option | Mechanic | Specialist Benefit | When It Matters |
|--------|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Free Push** | No Stress cost in domain | Saves Stress | Only when you roll Setback |
| **+1 Edge** | Cancel 1 Complication | Better vs. defended enemies | When Complications apply |
| **+1 Boon** | Bigger pool in domain | Less likely to fail | Every roll in domain |
| **Upgrade Push** | Better conversion | Cleaner successes | When you roll Setback/Conflict |

**Recommendation: Option 3 (+1 Boon)**

Why:
- ✅ Simple (happens at Step 2, baked into pool)
- ✅ Always relevant (affects every roll)
- ✅ Proactive benefit (roll better) vs. reactive (fix failures)
- ✅ No tracking (just roll more dice)
- ✅ Stacks with Edge from other sources

Free Push is too narrow (only matters on Setback). +1 Edge is complex (interacts with Step 7). Upgrade Push is too strong.

---

## Full Proposed System

### Character Building

**Remove "Expertise" as separate mechanic.**

**Traits automatically grant +1 Boon when invoked** (Step 2).

**No more tracking "Expertise: Heavy Blades, Expertise: Intimidation."**

Instead:
- Your Trait defines your specialty (Born Warrior, Street-Smart, etc.)
- When you invoke it, you get +1 Boon automatically
- This makes you roll better in your domain (less likely to need Push)

---

### Resolution Steps (9 Total)

1. **Frame** (GM sets Vantage, Stakes)
2. **Determine Pool** (Approach + Trait if invoked → auto +1 Boon)
3. **Apply Boons/Banes** (circumstantial, ±2 cap)
4. ~~Push Yourself~~
5. **Roll**
6. **Raw Outcome**
7. **Spend Edge** (cancel Complications)
8. **Apply Complications**
9. **Push Yourself** (mark 1 Stress to convert Setback → Conflict) ← NEW
10. **Final Outcome**

**9 steps instead of 10.**

---

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

✅ **Consolidates decision points:** One post-roll choice instead of two (pre-roll Push + passive Expertise)

✅ **More dramatic:** "I push through failure!" is stronger than "I spend Stress preemptively"

✅ **Simpler character building:** No separate Expertise tracking, Traits just grant +1 Boon

✅ **Democratic:** Everyone can Push, not just specialists

✅ **Fewer steps:** 10 → 9

✅ **Aligns with Grimwild:** Similar to their post-roll Push mechanic

---

### Cons

❌ **Less proactive:** Can't boost odds before rolling anymore

❌ **Might feel mandatory:** "Of course I spend 1 Stress to succeed" (every Setback becomes decision tax)

❌ **Specialists less differentiated:** If everyone can Push, specialists just roll better (less special feeling)

❌ **Edge remains complex:** Step 7 (Spend Edge) is still a separate post-roll layer

---

## Math Check: How Often Would You Push?

**Against 0 Complications (undefended enemy):**
- 3d6 (with Trait): Setback = 23%
- You'd Push ~23% of rolls (every Setback)

**Against 1 Complication (standard enemy):**
- 3d6 raw outcome: Conflict (26%) → Setback after Complication
- You'd Push on those 26% of rolls

**Against 2 Complications (defended enemy):**
- 3d6 raw outcome: Success (42%) → Conflict after Complications
- You wouldn't Push (Conflict still succeeds)
- Only Push if you rolled Conflict/Setback, which becomes Setback/Disaster

**So you'd Push on ~20-30% of rolls** (whenever you get Setback).

**Is that too often?** 

At 5 Stress capacity:
- If you Push 3 times in a combat (3 Stress)
- Plus take 1-2 Consequences (1-2 Stress)
- You'd fill your track and risk overflow

**The tension works.** It's not free—you can't Push every time.

---

## What About Assist?

**Current Assist:** Mark 1 Stress to grant ally +1 Boon (pre-roll)

**Should it change?**

**Option A: Keep as-is**
- Assist remains pre-roll (+1 Boon)
- Push Yourself is post-roll (convert Setback)
- Two different uses of Stress

**Option B: Make Assist post-roll too**
- Ally fails
- You mark 1 Stress to convert their Setback → Conflict
- "I've got your back!"

**Recommendation: Keep Assist as-is (pre-roll).**

Why:
- Assist is proactive ("I help you")
- Push is reactive ("I save myself")
- Different fictional moments
- Keeps some pre-roll Stress spending

---

## Comparison to Current System

### Current: Two Stress Spends, Two Conversions

**Pre-roll:**
- Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress for +1 Boon) — active, everyone
- Assist (mark 1 Stress for ally's +1 Boon) — active, everyone

**Post-roll:**
- Expertise (convert Setback → Conflict) — passive, specialists only

**Result:** 3 mechanics, 2 decision points

---

### Proposed: One Stress Spend, One Conversion

**Pre-roll:**
- Assist (mark 1 Stress for ally's +1 Boon) — active, everyone

**Post-roll:**
- Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress to convert Setback → Conflict) — active, everyone

**Passive benefit:**
- Trait grants +1 Boon automatically (no Stress cost) — specialists

**Result:** 3 mechanics, 2 decision points (same), but cleaner separation

---

## Final Recommendation

**Implement this change.**

**Why:**
1. ✅ Reduces steps (10 → 9)
2. ✅ More dramatic (post-roll Push is cinematic)
3. ✅ Aligns with Grimwild (both have post-roll resource spend)
4. ✅ Simplifies character building (Traits just grant +1 Boon, no Expertise tracking)
5. ✅ Consolidates decision points (one post-roll choice instead of pre-roll + passive)

**Compensate specialists with:**
- Traits auto-grant +1 Boon when invoked (built into Step 2)
- This makes them roll better, so they Push less often
- Feels like mastery: better odds, not just failure insurance

## New Rules Text

### Push Yourself (New Version)

> **Push Yourself**
> 
> When you roll a Setback, you may **mark 1 Stress** to convert it to **Conflict**. You succeed at your action, but face a Minor Consequence.
> 
> Describe how you push through—digging deep, forcing through pain, refusing to give up.
> 
> You cannot Push to convert Disaster (the outcome is too catastrophic).

---

### Trait Invocation (Updated)

> **Invoking Your Trait**
> 
> When a Trait is relevant to your action:
> 1. Declare which Trait you're invoking
> 2. Add its Approach die to your pool (if you didn't already)
> 3. Add **+1 Boon** automatically
> 4. You may use Special Effects (Enhanced Effect, Secondary Effect, Build Momentum)
> 
> This represents leaning on your core identity and specialty.

---

### Expertise (Removed)

~~Expertise: Convert Setback → Conflict in your domain~~

**Replaced by:** Trait invocation granting +1 Boon (everyone has this through Traits)

---

## What This Achieves

**Before:**
- Push Yourself (Step 4): Pre-roll, +1 Boon, everyone
- Expertise (Step 9): Post-roll, convert Setback, specialists only
- 10 steps total

**After:**
- Push Yourself (Step 9): Post-roll, convert Setback, everyone
- Traits (Step 2): Auto +1 Boon, everyone
- 9 steps total

**Result:**
- One fewer step
- More dramatic Push moments
- Simpler character building
- Aligned with Grimwild's model

---

## Remaining Complexity

Even with this change, you still have:
- Step 3: Boons/Banes (circumstantial)
- Step 7: Edge (cancel Complications)
- Step 8: Complications (apply tags)
- Step 9: Push Yourself (convert Setback)

**Edge + Complications are still your tactical layer.**

If you want to simplify FURTHER, those are next on the chopping block.

But this change (move Push, remove Expertise) is a clear win with minimal downside.
