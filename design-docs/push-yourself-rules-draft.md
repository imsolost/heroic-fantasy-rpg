# Push Yourself: Updated Rules Text & Talent Examples

## Core Rules Text

### Push Yourself (New Version - Talent Framework)

> **Push Yourself**
> 
> Some Talents require you to Push Yourself, marking 1 Stress to activate powerful effects. When you Push Yourself, describe how you're pushing beyond your normal limits—digging deep through pain, forcing past exhaustion, or tapping into reserves you didn't know you had.
> 
> **You can only Push Yourself once per Action Roll.** If you have multiple Talents that could trigger when you Push Yourself in the current situation, choose one to activate.
> 
> Talents that use this mechanic will say "you may Push Yourself" or "when you Push Yourself" in their text.

---

### What Happened to Baseline Push?

**Old rule (removed):**
> ~~When you make an Action Roll, you may mark 1 Stress to gain +1 Boon.~~

**Why it was removed:**
- Created resource overlap (both Stress and Momentum could boost your own rolls)
- Momentum (via Create an Opening) is now the primary way to proactively boost your odds
- Stress is for defense (absorbing hits), support (Assist), and special abilities (Talents)

---

## Assist (Unchanged)

> **Assist**
> 
> When an ally makes an Action Roll, you may mark 1 Stress to grant them +1 Boon before they roll. You don't need to take an action—describe how you're helping and mark the Stress.
> 
> **Requirements:**
> - You must be able to contribute fictionally (relevant positioning, tools, knowledge)
> - Multiple allies can Assist the same roll (each marking 1 Stress)
> - The +2 Boon cap still applies

---

## Create an Opening (Unchanged)

> **Create an Opening**
> 
> Before you make an Action Roll, you may spend 1 Momentum to Create an Opening. You gain **+1 Boon and +1 Edge**.
> 
> Describe how you're setting yourself up—finding the right angle, waiting for the perfect moment, exploiting a weakness, or creating a distraction.

---

## Expertise (Unchanged)

> **Expertise**
> 
> When your final outcome is Setback while acting within your domain of Expertise, convert it to Conflict. You succeed at your action, but face a Minor Consequence.
> 
> Your domains of Expertise are listed on your character sheet (Heavy Blades, Stealth, Intimidation, etc.).

---

## Talent Examples (Rewritten Format)

### Combat Talents

#### Surge (Offensive Power)

> **Surge**
> 
> When you Clash, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and mark 2 progress instead of 1 on the enemy's track.
> 
> You pour everything into a devastating strike, overwhelming your opponent's defenses through sheer force.

---

#### Defensive Stance (Reactive Defense)

> **Defensive Stance**
> 
> When you would mark Stress from a Consequence in combat, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 additional Stress) to gain +1 Boon on your next action and reduce the Consequence by one tier (Major → Minor, Minor → negated).
> 
> You absorb the blow and immediately counter-attack with renewed focus.

---

#### Battle Fury (Escalating Power)

> **Battle Fury**
> 
> When you Clash and have 3 or more Stress marked, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +2 Boons instead of +1 and ignore 1 Complication.
> 
> Pain fuels your rage. The more wounded you are, the more dangerous you become.

---

### Mobility Talents

#### Skirmisher (Mobile Fighter)

> **Skirmisher**
> 
> When you move in combat, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and move 1 additional zone. Enemies cannot block your movement this turn.
> 
> You dart through the battlefield with supernatural speed, flowing past obstacles and opponents alike.

---

#### Leap (Dramatic Movement)

> **Leap**
> 
> When you Defy to move (jumping, climbing, balancing), you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and automatically succeed if your final outcome would be Conflict. On Success or Triumph, you also land in a Position of advantage (gain 1 Edge on your next action).
> 
> You make an impossible leap, landing exactly where you need to be.

---

### Social Talents

#### Silver Tongue (Persuasion)

> **Silver Tongue**
> 
> When you Talk, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and choose one additional effect:
> - Plant a suggestion they'll act on later
> - Learn a secret they didn't mean to reveal
> - They trust you more than they should
> 
> Your words are carefully chosen, hitting exactly the right notes to sway hearts and minds.

---

#### Read the Room (Social Awareness)

> **Read the Room**
> 
> When you Sense in a social situation, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, ask 3 questions instead of 2, and the GM must answer one question about a character's hidden motive or secret.
> 
> You pick up on subtle cues others miss—the micro-expression, the nervous tell, the unspoken tension.

---

#### Intimidating Presence (Fear)

> **Intimidating Presence**
> 
> When you Clash (intimidation) or Talk (threatening), you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, also inflict Frightened 1 on your target.
> 
> Your presence alone makes them reconsider. They see violence in your eyes and fear what you're capable of.

---

### Knowledge & Investigation Talents

#### Eidetic Memory (Recall)

> **Eidetic Memory**
> 
> When you Know, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, the GM must give you one specific, actionable detail that normal knowledge wouldn't reveal (a name, a location, a weakness, a pattern).
> 
> You remember everything you've ever learned with perfect clarity, making connections others can't see.

---

#### Keen Eye (Investigation)

> **Keen Eye**
> 
> When you Sense to investigate a scene, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, you automatically notice any hidden clues or details (no additional roll needed), and ask your 2 questions.
> 
> You see what others overlook—the scuff mark, the displaced dust, the faint chemical smell.

---

### Magical Talents

#### Arcane Surge (Spellcasting)

> **Arcane Surge**
> 
> When you cast a spell, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and choose one:
> - Increase the spell's effect (as Enhanced Effect)
> - The spell also affects a secondary target
> - The spell's duration extends to the end of the scene
> 
> You channel more power than is safe, pushing your magic beyond its normal limits.

---

#### Ritual Focus (Extended Magic)

> **Ritual Focus**
> 
> When you perform a ritual (taking 10+ minutes), you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, the ritual also produces one additional benefit of your choice (knowledge, protection, communication, etc.).
> 
> You pour your will into the working, bending reality further than the ritual was designed for.

---

### Survival & Exploration Talents

#### Tracker (Wilderness)

> **Tracker**
> 
> When you Sense to track or navigate in the wilderness, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, you also learn one significant detail about what you're tracking (how many, how long ago, their condition, their destination).
> 
> You read the land like a book, seeing stories in broken branches and disturbed earth.

---

#### Endurance (Physical Resilience)

> **Endurance**
> 
> When you Defy physical exhaustion, harsh environments, or poison/disease, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, you also reduce one Affliction's size by 1 (minimum 0, clearing it).
> 
> You push through pain that would drop others, shrugging off effects through sheer toughness.

---

### Stealth & Infiltration Talents

#### Shadow Step (Stealth)

> **Shadow Step**
> 
> When you Defy to hide or move stealthily, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and ignore one Complication that would make the action harder (Vigilant enemy, bright lighting, lack of cover, etc.).
> 
> You become one with the shadows, slipping past even the most alert guards.

---

#### Quick Hands (Sleight of Hand)

> **Quick Hands**
> 
> When you Defy to pickpocket, plant an item, or palm something, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, even if someone is watching, they don't notice what you did.
> 
> Your hands move faster than the eye can follow. By the time they realize, it's already done.

---

### Leadership & Support Talents

#### Rallying Cry (Inspiration)

> **Rallying Cry**
> 
> When an ally you can see and hear makes an Action Roll, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to give them +1 Boon and grant them 1 Edge. This counts as your Push for this round (you can't use other Push Talents until your next turn).
> 
> Your words or actions inspire them to greatness, pushing them beyond what they thought possible.

---

#### Tactical Insight (Combat Leadership)

> **Tactical Insight**
> 
> When you Sense in combat to assess the battlefield, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon. On Success or better, you ask your 2 questions, and each ally gains 1 Edge on their next action as you shout tactical advice.
> 
> You see the flow of battle, spotting opportunities and calling out openings for your team.

## Design Notes for Talent Creation

### Every Push Talent Should Have:

1. **Trigger condition:** "When you [Action/situation]..."
2. **Activation cost:** "...you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress)..."
3. **Primary benefit:** "...to gain +1 Boon..."
4. **Special effect:** "...and [unique benefit]"
5. **Flavor text:** Brief description of what it looks like

---

### Guidelines for Special Effects:

**Keep effects roughly equivalent in power:**
- Mark extra progress (combat advantage)
- Gain positional advantage (1 Edge on next action)
- Secondary effect (two things from one roll)
- Inflict/clear Afflictions (condition management)
- Learn extra information (knowledge advantage)
- Bypass one Complication (tactical bypass)

**Don't go overboard:**
- ❌ "Mark 3 progress and gain 2 Edge and ignore all Complications"
- ✅ "Mark 2 progress instead of 1"

**Make it feel special, not broken.**

---

### Contextual vs Universal Talents

**Narrow context = stronger effect:**
> "When you Clash with a sword against an armored opponent, Push Yourself to gain +1 Boon and ignore Resilient."

**Broad context = moderate effect:**
> "When you Clash, Push Yourself to gain +1 Boon and mark 2 progress instead of 1."

**Universal = weakest effect:**
> "When you make any Action Roll, Push Yourself to gain +1 Boon and... [minor benefit]"

**Narrower is more interesting** - creates specialization, encourages varied Talent choices.

## Player Reference Card

### How to Boost Your Odds

**Before Your Roll:**
1. **Circumstantial Boons** - Position yourself well, use right tools (GM grants)
2. **Create an Opening** - Spend 1 Momentum for +1 Boon + 1 Edge
3. **Assist** - Ally marks 1 Stress to grant you +1 Boon
4. **Push Yourself (if you have a Talent)** - Mark 1 Stress to activate Talent benefit

**Boons cap at +2** (max 4d6 pool)

**Edge has no cap** (but excess Edge is wasted if there aren't enough Complications)

## FAQ

### Q: Can I Push Yourself if I don't have a relevant Talent?

**A: No.** Push Yourself only works if you have a Talent that applies to the current situation. Without a Talent, you can't Push.

---

### Q: What if I have two Talents that both apply?

**A: Choose one.** You can only Push Yourself once per Action Roll, so you activate one Talent.

**Example:** You're attacking with a sword. You have both Surge (mark 2 progress) and Quick Strike (act first). You can Push to activate one, but not both.

---

### Q: Can I Push Yourself on the same action where I Create an Opening?

**A: Yes.** They're different resources (Stress vs Momentum) and different mechanics. You could Create an Opening (Momentum) for +1 Boon + 1 Edge, AND Push Yourself (Stress via Talent) for +1 Boon + special effect.

**But you'd be spending 1 Momentum + 1 Stress** on one roll, which is expensive. Choose wisely.

---

### Q: Can I Push Yourself after I see the result?

**A: No (usually).** Most Talents say "when you [make the roll]", which means you declare before rolling. The cost is paid upfront.

**Exception:** Talents that specifically trigger after the roll (like Defensive Stance) would allow post-roll activation. The Talent text will make this clear.

---

### Q: Can multiple people Push Yourself to Assist the same ally?

**A: Each person can either Assist OR Push Yourself, not both.**

- Assisting = marking your Stress for their Boon (simple support)
- Push Yourself = activating your own Talent (benefits yourself, usually)

**Exception:** Rallying Cry specifically lets you Push to help an ally. That's the Talent's special effect.

---

### Q: Does Push Yourself count as spending Stress?

**A: Yes, you mark Stress (it fills a box).** It's not "spending" in the sense of reducing a pool, but you are voluntarily marking Stress, which counts toward filling your track and potentially causing overflow.

## Summary of Changes

### What Changed

**Removed:** Push Yourself as baseline "+1 Boon for 1 Stress" (universal option)

**Kept:** Push Yourself as Talent activation framework (contextual, once per action)

**Why:** 
- Cleaner resource separation (Momentum = proactive offense, Stress = reactive defense)
- Fewer decision points (no more "should I Push?" every single roll)
- Talents become more important (your main way to spend Stress proactively)
- 9 steps → 8 steps

---

### What Players Do Now

**To boost your own odds:**
1. Create an Opening (Momentum) - primary proactive option
2. Talents (Push Yourself) - contextual power spikes
3. Position/tools (circumstantial Boons from GM)

**To support allies:**
1. Assist (Stress) - unchanged

**To absorb danger:**
1. Stress track - unchanged (marks from Consequences)

---

### What GMs Need to Know

**Players can no longer:**
- Mark 1 Stress for +1 Boon on any roll

**Players can now:**
- Only Push Yourself if they have a Talent that applies
- Talents grant +1 Boon + special effect when they Push

**Momentum is now more important:**
- It's the primary way to boost your own odds proactively
- Consider being slightly more generous with Momentum grants for clever plans
- Or increase Momentum capacity if it feels too scarce

**Stress is now more defensive:**
- Marks from getting hit (primary source)
- Marks from Assist (support role)
- Marks from Push Yourself (special abilities)
- Should fill more slowly, reflecting danger rather than player spending

---

## Next Steps

1. **Update all existing Talents** that reference Push Yourself to new format
2. **Playtest for 3-5 sessions** to gauge Momentum economy
3. **Watch for:**
   - Do players feel starved for Momentum?
   - Are Talents clear about when they can be used?
   - Does Stress fill too slowly (less tension)?
   - Do characters without many Talents feel weak?

4. **Adjust if needed:**
   - Increase Momentum capacity (3 → 4)
   - Add Momentum sources (Talents, session start)
   - Clarify Talent wording
   - Revert change if it doesn't work

This is a conservative change with clear benefits and low risk. Perfect starting point for simplification.
