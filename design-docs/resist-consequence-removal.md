# Resist Consequence Removal

## Rationale

Removed Resist Consequence to simplify Momentum spending and enforce proactive gameplay.

### Key Issues with Resist Consequence

1. **Overlap with Edge** — Create an Opening provides Edge, which already prevents consequences by canceling Complications. Resist Consequence did the same work reactively.

2. **Encouraged hoarding** — Optimal play became "save Momentum for emergencies" rather than spending proactively.

3. **Reactive vs. Proactive** — Grimwold's design philosophy: all resource spending happens before rolling. You commit, roll, and accept the outcome. No post-roll mitigation.

4. **Allies can help** — Create an Opening now works for allies, so teammates can spend their Momentum to help you succeed (replacing the need for individual safety nets).

### What Resist Consequence Did

1. **Mitigated consequences against 0-Complication enemies** — Roll Conflict → Resist → Success (spend 1 Momentum to eliminate Minor Consequence)

2. **Softened catastrophic rolls** — Roll Setback → Resist → Conflict (Major → Minor Consequence)

### Why Removing It Works

1. **Edge already mitigates consequences proactively** — Spend Momentum for Edge before rolling. Against enemies with Complications, this prevents consequence escalation.

2. **Chip damage is fine** — Rolling Conflict against minions and taking 1 Stress is reasonable. Makes minions relevant without requiring Momentum spend.

3. **Setbacks should hurt** — Rolling all 1-3s is a genuinely bad roll. Expertise converts it to Conflict for specialists. For non-specialists, consequences are the price of failure.

4. **Proactive spending creates better gameplay** — "Spend before you know the outcome" creates commitment and drama. "Save for emergencies" creates defensive hoarding.

5. **Simplifies decision space** — Two Momentum spends (Create an Opening, Seize Spotlight) instead of three. All spending is pre-roll.

## New Momentum Economy

**Momentum Spends:**
1. **Create an Opening** (1 Momentum) — +1 Boon + 1 Edge, for self or ally (pre-roll)
2. **Seize Spotlight** (1 Momentum) — Act immediately

**All spending is pre-roll commitment.** No reactive mitigation. Commit, roll, accept the outcome.

## Impact on Gameplay

1. **Players must commit before knowing outcome** — More dramatic, more heroic. No safety net for bad rolls.

2. **Edge becomes THE defensive mechanic** — Want to avoid consequences against tough enemies? Spend Momentum for Edge before rolling.

3. **Teamwork emphasis** — Allies can Create an Opening for you (spend their Momentum to give you +1 Boon + 1 Edge).

4. **Momentum economy adjustments become more important** — With no reactive spend, players need enough Momentum capacity and sources to afford proactive spending. Current plan: increase cap to 5, add starting Momentum (2), add Momentum-granting Talents.

5. **Matches Grimwold philosophy** — All resource spending is pre-roll. No post-roll modification except Expertise (which is automatic, not a choice).

## Future Considerations

- Monitor whether removing the reactive option makes gameplay too punishing
- Ensure Momentum sources are plentiful enough to support proactive spending
- Consider whether Expertise alone is sufficient safety net for catastrophic rolls
