# Updated Core Rules: Final Draft

## Changes Summary

**Removed:**
1. Push Yourself (baseline) - No longer grants +1 Boon for marking 1 Stress
2. Assist - No longer a separate mechanic

**Modified:**
1. Push Yourself - Now Talent-only activation framework
2. Create an Opening - Now works for self OR ally

**Kept Unchanged:**
- Expertise
- Traits
- Complications + Edge
- All other mechanics

**Note:** Momentum economy adjustments (capacity, sources) will be addressed separately.

---

## Push Yourself (New Version)

**Push Yourself**

Some Talents require you to Push Yourself, marking 1 Stress to activate powerful effects. When you do, describe how you're pushing beyond your normal limits—digging deep through pain, forcing past exhaustion, or tapping into reserves you didn't know you had.

**You can only Push Yourself once per Action Roll.** If you have multiple Talents that could trigger when you Push Yourself in the current situation, choose one to activate.

Talents that use this mechanic will say "you may Push Yourself" or "when you Push Yourself" in their text.

**Without a relevant Talent, you cannot Push Yourself.** Push Yourself has no baseline effect—it only works through Talents.

---

## Create an Opening (Updated)

**Create an Opening**

Before any Action Roll, you may spend 1 Momentum to Create an Opening. The character making the roll (whether yourself or an ally) gains **+1 Boon and +1 Edge**.

Describe how you're creating the advantage:
- **For yourself:** Finding the right angle, waiting for the perfect moment, exploiting a weakness
- **For an ally:** Shouting tactical advice, creating a distraction, revealing an opening

**Only one character can Create an Opening for a given roll.** Multiple people cannot stack this benefit.

---

## Assist (Removed)

~~When an ally makes an Action Roll, you may mark 1 Stress to grant them +1 Boon.~~

**This mechanic has been removed.** Use Create an Opening to help allies instead (spend 1 Momentum to grant them +1 Boon + 1 Edge).

---

## Expertise (Unchanged)

**Expertise**

When your final outcome is Setback while acting within your domain of Expertise, convert it to Conflict. You succeed at your action, but face a Minor Consequence.

Your domains of Expertise are listed on your character sheet (Heavy Blades, Stealth, Intimidation, etc.).

---

## Teamwork (Updated)

When 3 or more PCs work together on a single task, one person makes the primary roll. The GM decides who rolls based on the fiction—sometimes the most skilled (lifting a boulder together), sometimes the least skilled (sneaking past guards as a group).

One other character may Create an Opening for the roller (spend 1 Momentum to grant them +1 Boon and +1 Edge).

The entire group shares the consequences of the roll.

---

## How to Boost Your Odds (Updated List)

**Before Your Roll:**

1. **Circumstantial Boons** - Good positioning, right tools, favorable conditions (GM grants)

2. **Create an Opening** - You or an ally spends 1 Momentum → +1 Boon + 1 Edge
   - Only one per roll
   - Works for self or ally

3. **Push Yourself** - Mark 1 Stress to activate a Talent (if you have one that applies)
   - Only once per action
   - Grants +1 Boon + Talent's special effect

**Boons cap at +2** (max 4d6 pool)

**Edge has no cap** (but excess is wasted if there aren't enough Complications)

---

## Resource Usage Summary

**Momentum:**
- **Create an Opening** (before any roll, for self or ally) → +1 Boon + 1 Edge
- **Resist Consequence** (after outcome) → reduce severity by one tier
- Gain from: Triumphs, Build Momentum (Special Effect), GM grants

**Stress:**
- Marks from: Consequences (automatic)
- Marks from: Push Yourself (activate Talents)
- Clears via: Short Rest (1 hour), Long Rest (overnight)

---

## Resolution Steps (Final)

1. **Frame** (GM sets Vantage, Stakes)
2. **Determine Pool** (Approach + Trait if invoked)
3. **Apply Boons/Banes** (circumstantial, ±2 cap)
4. **Roll**
5. **Determine Raw Outcome** (Triumph/Success/Conflict/Setback)
6. **Apply Complications** (using Edge if available)
7. **Apply Expertise** (convert Setback if in domain)
8. **Apply Final Outcome** (Consequences, mark tracks)

**7 effective steps** (Push Yourself and Assist no longer separate steps in resolution)

---

## Talent Format (Standard Template)

When writing Talents that use Push Yourself:

**[Talent Name]**

When you [trigger condition], you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and [special effect].

[Flavor text describing what it looks like]

**Examples:**
- **Surge:** When you Clash, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and mark 2 progress instead of 1.
- **Shadow Step:** When you Defy to hide or move stealthily, you may Push Yourself (mark 1 Stress) to gain +1 Boon and ignore one Complication that would make the action harder.

## Migration Notes

**Players need to know:**
1. Can't mark Stress for generic +1 Boon anymore
2. Can't Assist separately—use Create an Opening instead (costs Momentum, grants more)
3. Push Yourself only works if you have a Talent

**GMs need to know:**
1. Create an Opening can be used for allies now
2. Only one Opening per roll (not stackable)
3. Teamwork is now Momentum-based (more strategic, less spammable)

**Future work:**
- Momentum economy adjustments (capacity, starting amount, sources)
- These will be addressed in a separate update
